Photos lets you attach pictures to a person's record in Community and choose where each one shows. One photo can be the picture members see in the directory. One can be the picture on your public website for people listed there. This page covers adding, choosing and removing photos, and the cases where a photo is never shown.

## Turn it on

1. Open "Administration", then "Features". See [Turning features on and off](/help/turning-features-on-and-off).
2. Turn on "Photos of people". It is off by default.

The link to the photos page is on the edit page for a person. If "Community: add people and record roll actions" is off, that page says member management isn't turned on and shows no "Photos" link, so turn Community on as well. See [Adding people and recording members](/help/adding-people).

If you turn "Photos of people" off while photos are chosen, the page asks "Turn off Photos of people?" and says how many photos are chosen. Choose "Turn it off" to confirm or "Keep it on" to leave it on. Turning it off hides every photo, including on your public website. Nothing is deleted, and turning it on again restores them. Browsers that already saved a photo can keep showing it for up to a day. While it is off, the photos page says "Photos of people aren't turned on" for your congregation and who can turn them on.

## Add photos

1. Open "Community" and choose a person, then "Edit person".
2. Select the link "Photos". The page is headed "Photos". It has a link "Back to edit person". It shows how many photos the person has, such as "0 of 10 photos", and "No photos yet" with "Add the first photo below." when there are none.
3. Under "Add a photo", select "Choose a photo". Pick a picture, or take one with your camera. The page says: "Pick a picture or take one with your camera. JPEG, PNG or WebP. It is resized here and stored without location information."

The page says "Photo added." For a person's first photo it adds "It's now" the person's name "directory photo." A person can have up to ten photos in your congregation. Photos belong to the congregation: a person who belongs to two congregations has photos set up in each.

The same picture cannot be added twice: the page says "That picture is already in" the person's name "photos." A picture of type JPEG, PNG or WebP works. A file of another type is refused with the message "That file isn't a JPEG, PNG or WebP picture. If it's an iPhone photo, pick it from your photo library or convert it to JPEG first."

The first photo you add for a person becomes their directory photo automatically. A photo never becomes the website photo on its own.

## Two places a photo shows

Under "Where photos show" the page tells you what members and the website see now. Each photo is a tile. A chip on the tile says where it is used: "Directory", "Website" or "Directory, cleared for website".

**The directory.** Members who can see the person in the directory see the photo marked "Directory", or the person's initials if there is none. Select "Use in directory" on a tile to choose it. The dialog "Use this photo in the directory?" has a checkbox about the person's permission to also use the photo on the public website. Tick it if the person agrees. That clears the photo for the website as well.

**The public website.** The website shows the photo marked "Website". If there is none, it can use the directory photo, but only when that photo was cleared for the website. Select "Use on website" to choose a website photo. The dialog "Use this photo on the public website?" says that anyone on the internet can see it, and that search engines and web archives may keep copies that removing the photo later cannot recall. The button stays off until you tick the box saying you have the person's permission. That box is your statement as staff. PresbyPortal records it. It is not the person's own consent.

Other tile buttons:

- "Clear for website" lets the directory photo also serve the website. A dialog with the same permission box asks you to confirm.
- "Stop using in directory" and "Stop using on website" take a photo out of one place after you confirm "Stop using". "Stop using on website" on a photo that is also the cleared directory photo changes nothing on the website, and the dialog says so. To keep a photo in the directory but off the website, use "Stop using in directory", then choose it again without ticking the box.
- "View larger" opens the picture, and "Back to all photos" returns.

Each dialog says what the choice changes before you confirm, and has a "Cancel" button.

## When a photo is on the website

A photo appears on the website only when all of these are true:

- the person is listed on the public website. Listing is set separately, under Staff, Officers or Groups, by whoever manages those. See [Your congregation's website](/help/your-congregations-website);
- a website photo is chosen, or the directory photo was cleared for the website;
- the website is published.

The page shows lines for this such as "Website: the photo marked "Website" below." or "Website: no photo chosen, so the website shows none."

## Photos that are never shown

- A photo of a person who appears to be under 18. A person appears to be under 18 from a recorded birth date, a birth year that could make them under 18, or being marked as a child in their household. The photos page says the person appears to be under 18 and that photos of children can't be added yet. Existing photos are not shown in the directory or on the website. A person with no birth date and no child marking is treated as an adult.
- A photo the person has hidden. The page says "Directory: not shown, because" the person "has hidden their photo", and the same for the website. The person's card shows initials.
- On the website, a record that was merged into another.

## Remove a photo

Select "Remove" on a tile and confirm "Remove photo" in the dialog "Remove this photo?". The photo is no longer used anywhere. The image file stays in storage, and it cannot be erased from here. Browsers that saved it can keep showing it for up to a day. Copies kept by search engines or web archives cannot be recalled. The dialog tells you where the photo was in use, such as that the person's initials show in the directory instead.

## What is not here

Members cannot add their own photo. Photos of children, photos of households, uploading many photos at once, photos in the Community lists and a tool to crop a picture are not available. The directory shows a square thumbnail taken from the picture, toward its top.
