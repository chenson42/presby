An invitation is an email with a link. The link lets you create a PresbyPortal account, or use the one you have, and connects it to your place in a congregation. An invitation to be a congregation's first administrator or its treasurer also gives you that role when you accept. This page walks through it from the email to your congregation's page.

## The email

The email comes from PresbyPortal. Its heading is "You're invited to PresbyPortal" and it has a button, "Accept the invitation". The email says on what date the link stops working.

- An invitation from your congregation says that your congregation has invited you to sign in. It names the congregation, and usually the person who sent it.
- An invitation to be a congregation's first administrator or its treasurer says that a PresbyPortal administrator invited you.

The link works once, and only for the address the email was sent to. If you were not expecting the email, you can ignore it. Nothing happens unless you accept.

## The invitation page

The link opens a page headed "You're invited". It says which congregation invited you, shows part of the address the email went to, such as `i***@example.invalid`, and shows the date the invitation expires. It does not show the whole address.

What the page offers depends on whether you have an account.

### If you are new

Under "Create your account", fill in "First name", "Last name", "Password" and "Confirm password". The page asks for a password of at least 8 characters that you do not use anywhere else. A button beside the password shows or hides what you type. Then select "Create account".

The page then says "Your account is ready". It does not sign you in. Select "Sign in" and use the email address the invitation was sent to and the password you just chose.

### If you already have an account

Under "Already have an account?", select "Sign in to accept", sign in with the account that uses the address the invitation was sent to, and you come back to the invitation. The page says who you are accepting as and where. If that is not you, do not accept: contact the person who invited you. Otherwise select "Accept invitation". You go on to the congregation's page.

Two other cases can appear:

- If you are signed in as a different account, the page says "You're signed in as another account" and offers "Sign out to accept".
- If your account uses two-factor sign-in, the page asks you to verify your code first and offers "Verify and continue". When you have verified, you come back to the invitation. See [Using your code to sign in](/help/using-your-code).

If you sign in with the address the invitation was sent to and you are not yet connected to a congregation, the invitation can also appear on its own page. That page is headed "You're invited to" and the congregation's name, or "You have" a number "invitations" if there is more than one. Select "Accept the invitation" there.

## If the link does not work

If the link was already used, has expired, or was replaced by a newer one, the page is headed "This link isn't active". It says the same thing whatever the reason: "If you already accepted it, you can sign in. Otherwise ask whoever invited you to send a new one." Select "Go to sign in" if you have accepted already. If whoever invited you cannot help, [send the PresbyPortal team a message](/help/contact).

## Two-factor sign-in

Whether you are asked to use two-factor sign-in depends on your account, your role and your congregation. See [Who is asked for a code](/help/who-is-asked-for-a-code). If you are asked, PresbyPortal takes you to the set-up page when you sign in, before it lets you into your congregation's pages. See [Setting up two-factor sign-in](/help/setting-up-two-factor).

## Where you land

When you have signed in and, if asked, set up two-factor sign-in, you land on your congregation's page if you belong to one congregation. If you belong to more than one, you land on your home page. See [Your home page and switching congregations](/help/home-and-switching-congregations).

If you are a congregation's first administrator, [Your first day as an administrator](/help/first-day-as-administrator) is the next page to read.
