The Officers page records who holds each office in your congregation. Recording a ruling elder, an installed pastor, a co-pastor or an associate pastor also seats them on the Session. Members of the Session decide who is received onto the roll.

## Open the Officers page

Choose the "Officers" tile under "Governance & Courts" on your congregation's page. The page is headed "Officers". Under "Current roster" it lists each officer with the office, the start date and an "End term" button. A new congregation has none, and the page says "No officers recorded yet".

## Recording an officer term

1. Under "Add an officer term", choose the person.
2. Choose the office: "Ruling Elder", "Installed Pastor", "Co-Pastor", "Associate Pastor", "Deacon", "Moderator of the Board of Deacons", "Clerk of Session", "Moderator", "Treasurer" or "Trustee".
3. Enter the start date. "Elected on", "Installed on", "Class year" and "Minute reference" are optional. The page says the minute reference is the only paper trail that confirms the election or installation happened.
4. Choose "Add officer term". The message is "Officer term recorded."

A term that starts on a later date is recorded, and the person joins the Session on that date.

Recording an officer does not change what the person can do in PresbyPortal, apart from two seats: the Session, and the Moderator of the Board of Deacons. Other access is given under "Roles". See [Roles: who can do what](/help/roles-who-can-do-what).

### A deacon's district

When you choose "Deacon", the form says whether districts are turned on. If Parish management is off, it says "Districts aren't turned on yet" and that an administrator can turn on Parish management under Features.

### Pastors

A pastor's membership is held by the presbytery, so a pastor is recorded as an officer without a roll membership. If the pastor is not in the list of people, add them from the "Staff" page first. If the person is on your congregation's roll, the page refuses: it says a pastor cannot also be recorded on the roll here.

An installed pastor, a co-pastor and an associate pastor count as members of the Session.

## Ending a term

Choose "End term" beside the officer. The dialog asks for an end date and a reason: "Completed", "Resigned", "Removed" or "Deceased". It says that the term stays on record and is not deleted, and that any Session or diaconate access ends the day the term does. Terms are ended, never deleted. A person's officer history lists every term with its dates and reason.

## The Session roster is built from terms

You do not add people to the Session directly. Under "Groups", the "Automatic rosters" section lists "Session" and "Board of Deacons", and says they are generated automatically from officer terms and the membership roll. To change who is on the Session, record or end an officer term.

## Why "Pending approvals" says no one can approve

A new member waits for the Session. Until a member of the Session is in effect and has a PresbyPortal account, "Pending approvals" says no one can approve yet. Record at least one ruling elder, installed pastor, co-pastor or associate pastor, and make sure they have an account. The banner and what to do about each case are in [Adding people and recording members](/help/adding-people).

A member of the Session needs a PresbyPortal account to approve. Today the PresbyPortal team sets that up: send a support ticket. See [Getting help](/help/getting-help).

## Recording yourself

You can record yourself as a ruling elder. When you choose the person who is you, the form says you are recording yourself as a member of the Session, and that this is allowed. The roster then marks the term "Self-recorded", and anything you approve is marked "Self-approved".
