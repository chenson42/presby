A role is a named set of things a person may do in your congregation, such as adding people or turning features on and off. You give a role to a person, and you take it away when they no longer need it.

## Open the Roles page

Choose "Administration", then "Roles". The page has three parts: "Who holds what", "Grant a role" and "Role catalog".

A new congregation has six roles.

| Role | Who holds it at first |
|---|---|
| "Founding Administrator" | You |
| "Stated Clerk" | You |
| "Member" | The "Active Membership" group |
| "Calendar viewer" | The "All Current Records" group |
| "Session member" | The "Session" group |
| "Moderator of the Board of Deacons" | The "Board of Deacons Moderator" group |

A group holds the last four, so whoever is in that group has the role. The groups fill by themselves. "Active Membership" holds people with member standing: those on the current roll, regular attenders and staff records. "All Current Records" holds everyone with a current record, including visitors and people marked inactive or moved away. "Session" and "Board of Deacons Moderator" fill from officer terms. See [Officers and the Session](/help/officers-and-the-session).

## Giving a role

1. Under "Grant a role", choose "A person" or "A group".
2. Choose the role, then the person.
3. Choose "Grant role". The message is "Role granted."

The role starts straight away. The "Moderator of the Board of Deacons" role is held through that seat. If you try to give it by hand, the page says it is held through the seat and cannot be granted here.

## Taking a role away

Choose "Revoke" beside the row. The dialog asks you to confirm, and says the grant ends and stays on record: it is not deleted. A message confirms it.

You cannot take away the last way anyone has to give roles. The page says "Revoking this would leave nobody able to grant or revoke roles at this organization. Contact support if you need to change this."

## Creating and editing a role

The "Role catalog" lists each role with its number of permissions, its holders and its status. "Founding Administrator" and "Stated Clerk" show "Edit". "Member", "Calendar viewer", "Session member" and "Moderator of the Board of Deacons" are "Constitutional" and cannot be edited.

- To make your own role, choose "Create role". Give it a key (lowercase letters, numbers and underscores, starting with a letter) and a name, and tick what it allows. The list is grouped by how sensitive the information is: directory, financial, and pastoral, demographic and medical. Each item has a short description. Choose "Create role". The message is "Role created."
- To start from a stock role, use "Or adopt a template". The templates are "Committee Chair", "Giving Recorder", "Hiring Committee", "Personnel Administrator", "Stated Clerk" and "Treasurer". Choose one, then "Adopt template". The message is "Role adopted from template."
- To change a role, choose "Edit", change the ticks and choose "Save changes". The change applies at once to everyone who holds the role.
- To retire a role, choose "Deactivate role" under "Danger zone". The dialog says it ends every current grant of the role, and that the role and its ended grants stay on record. The role stays in the catalog marked "Deactivated".

You cannot save changes that would leave nobody able to create or edit roles. The page says "Removing this would leave nobody able to create or edit roles at this organization. Contact support if you need to change this."

## You can only give what you hold

A role can include only permissions you hold yourself. If you tick one you do not hold, the page refuses. For a new role it says "You can't create a role with permissions you don't hold yourself" and names the permission. For an existing role it says "You can't add permissions you don't hold yourself". Taking a permission away never needs this check.

## What a Founding Administrator can and cannot do

A Founding Administrator can:

- turn features on and off;
- add and change people, households and contact details, and record how people are connected;
- record officers and the Session;
- create and manage groups, and record staff positions;
- give roles, take them away, and create and edit roles from what they hold;
- ask the PresbyPortal team for help with a ticket;
- write and publish job openings, once "Job openings" is turned on.

A Founding Administrator cannot, on the first day:

- add or change events, or event types, because the role that allows it is not one a Founding Administrator holds. The "Manage events" page says "You don't have permission to manage events";
- change the congregation's colours, type pairing or logo. The "Branding" page says "You don't have permission to manage branding";
- give either of those to anyone else. The role form refuses, as above;
- turn on "Website domain", which is not on the Features page.

For these, the PresbyPortal team sets things up. {{contactAdmin}} When the team gives you a role that allows them, you can give the same role to others.
