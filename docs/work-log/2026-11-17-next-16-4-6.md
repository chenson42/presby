## Why

- **Two moderate advisories** against 16.4.3 (GHSA-mfr8-8qww-v76h, GHSA-vc7v-qv4w-wrv8), fixed in 16.4.5. Both concern `'use cache'` / `unstable_cache`, which presby does not use, so exposure was nil; the bump clears the audit lines.
- **Local development is blocked.** Since 2026-11-17, 16.4.3's start-up upgrade notice ("SecurityFatalError") stops every local `next dev`, including with `CI=1` and telemetry disabled, so no local e2e or browser check can run. CI was unaffected.

Operator asked for this on 2026-11-17 ("patch next.js").
