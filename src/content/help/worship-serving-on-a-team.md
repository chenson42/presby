When someone asks you to serve on a worship team, you answer in PresbyPortal. This page covers where the request arrives, how to answer it, and how to save dates you can't serve.

## Before you start

Your congregation has to use Worship. An administrator turns it on under "Features", and it is off until they do. While it is off, the "Worship" tile is not on your congregation's page, and a link from an email says "Worship isn't turned on for" your congregation. See [Turning features on and off](/help/turning-features-on-and-off). You also need a sign-in connected to your record. If you can't sign in, tell whoever asked you to serve. A leader can record your answer for you.

## When you're asked

You get an email from your congregation listing the dates and positions. It does not say who asked you and does not name anyone else. Choose the button and sign in if you are asked to. With one request waiting, the page "Your request to serve" opens. With several, "Worship" opens and lists them. PresbyPortal cannot tell whether an email reached your inbox or was read. If you expected a request and none came, it may not have been sent yet, or the email may not have reached you. Open "Worship" to see any request waiting for you.

Choose "Accept" or "Decline". If you decline, you can choose a "Reason (optional)": Away, Work, Family or Other. People who schedule worship can see it, and PresbyPortal does not show it to anyone else.

You can change your answer until the service starts. A request you declined shows "I can serve after all" on your schedule.

If the page says "This request is no longer open", the service was cancelled, it has started, or the leader took the request back. Choose "Go to my schedule".

## Your schedule

Choose the "Worship" tile. The page lists "Waiting for your answer", "You're scheduled" and "You said you can't serve", each with the date, your team and your position. Under "Your teams" you can open a team to see who is on it and which positions each person can fill.

Reminders are emailed once a night. Each request still waiting for you, and each position you are scheduled in soon, can get its own reminder, so you may receive more than one. A reminder about a request still waiting for you opens that request. A reminder about a position you are scheduled in opens "Worship". They can arrive late in the evening. You get none for a request you declined, or for a service that was cancelled.

## Dates I can't serve

Choose "Dates I can't serve". Pick "From" and "To", optionally a reason, and choose "Save dates". When people who schedule worship choose who to ask, they are warned about a date you saved. They do not see your reason. Saving dates does not cancel anything you have already agreed to: if you are already confirmed on one of the days, the page says so, and you tell the person who asked you. Dates that touch or overlap are joined into one.

## If you are under 18

PresbyPortal does not email anyone whose record shows they may be under 18. A leader asks a parent or guardian and records the answer for you. A record with no birth date and no child marking is treated as an adult, so ask your church office to correct it if that is wrong.
