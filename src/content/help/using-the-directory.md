The directory lists the people of your congregation. This page explains how to search it, how to read a household, and who to ask when an entry is wrong.

If you see "You don't have permission to view the directory.", ask an administrator at your congregation.

## Opening the directory

Open your congregation's page and select "Directory". The page has two views, "Members" and "Households".

## Finding a person

1. Under "Search the directory", type part of a name, an email address or a phone number.
2. Select "Search". The list shows how many people matched.
3. To narrow the list, choose a "Status": All, Active, Baptized, Affiliate or Other participant. The status is the one recorded for the person on the roll.

Select a person to open their page. It shows their contact details, their household and the people in it.

Search looks only at what a card shows. A hidden email address or phone number never matches, and a person who hides it can still be found by name.

## Households

Select "Households" to see one card for each household, with the number of members. A household that has a deacon names the deacon. Select a household to open its page.

## Who you do not see, and what is hidden

- Details a person has marked private do not show. Email, phone, address, birthday and photo can each be hidden on their own.
- Some people are not listed at all: people marked hidden from the directory, visitors, people marked inactive or moved away who are not on the roll, and people whose connection to the congregation has ended.
- Cards show a person's initials in a circle, not a photo.

## Changing an entry

You cannot change anyone's entry from the directory, including your own. Ask a church administrator. They change a person's record in "Community".
