This page is for two kinds of people. The first is the chair or leader of a group that receives reimbursement requests, who approves them. The second is the treasury team, who record payments and set Reimbursements up. If you are the person asking to be paid back, read [Asking to be reimbursed](/help/reimbursements) instead.

## Approve a request

If you are the chair or leader of a group that can receive requests, a request charged to your group waits for you.

1. You get an email, "A reimbursement request needs your approval". It has a button, "Open the request". It does not say what the request is for. Sign in to see the details.
2. You can also find it on the Reimbursements page, under "Waiting for your approval" and a count.
3. Open the request. Read the amount, what it was for and the receipts.
4. Select "Approve". The dialog states the amount, who is paid and the group it is charged to. Select "Approve" again to confirm. A message says "Approved." and the request goes to the treasurer for payment.
5. To say no, select "Send back". Choose "Needs changes" if the requester can fix it and send it again, or "Not approved" if the request should end there. Write a reason in the box labelled "Reason (the requester will see this)". It is required.

Nobody approves their own request. The person who approves a request cannot also record its payment. If the request changed after you opened it, the page says "This request changed after you opened it. Review it again." If someone already decided, it says so with the date.

## The treasurer view

If you handle reimbursements, the Reimbursements page has a "Treasurer view" button. It opens a page headed "Reimbursement requests". You can also reach it from "Administration", under "Giving & Finance", on the "Reimbursement requests" tile. A person who is not on the treasury team sees a message that the page is for the people who handle reimbursements.

The page has tabs.

| Tab | What it lists |
|---|---|
| "Waiting for payment" | Approved requests that need a payment recorded. |
| "Needs treasurer approval" | Requests that no one in the group can approve. |
| "In progress" | Requests still being decided. |
| "Paid" | Requests with a payment recorded. |
| "All" | Every request that has been sent in. |

You can filter by group ("Charged to") and by the dates requests were sent ("Submitted from" and "Submitted to"), then select "Filter". "Export CSV" downloads the requests as a spreadsheet file. The file covers every request that has been sent in, not only the tab you are on or the group you chose under "Charged to". The dates you enter do narrow it. It can hold up to 20,000 requests: if the download says "Narrow the date range: that export is more than 20,000 requests.", enter dates and try again. Every export is recorded.

Everyone who views, pays or manages requests signs in with two-factor sign-in. See [Using your code to sign in](/help/using-your-code).

## Record a payment

PresbyPortal records the payment. It does not send money or ask for bank details. Pay the person or the vendor the way your church does, then record it.

1. Open a request that is "Approved, waiting for payment".
2. Select "Record payment".
3. Under "How was it paid?", choose "Check", "ACH", "Cash" or "Other". For a check, enter the "Check number". You can add a "Reference (optional)" and a "Note (optional)".
4. Enter the "Date paid". It cannot be before the day of the expense or after today.
5. Select "Record payment". A message says "Payment recorded."

The requester sees "Paid on" and the date. They do not see how it was paid.

The page may refuse, and says why:

- "A different person must record this payment." The person who approved the request cannot record its payment.
- "Another person who can record payments should do this, because you entered this request or are being paid by it." Someone other than the requester records it. If you are the only person who can record payments at your church, you can record a payment on your own request, and the request is marked "Marked as paid by the person who entered or is paid by the request."
- "Add a receipt or waive it before paying." A request needs a receipt before payment. To pay without one, select "Waive receipt". A dialog, "Waive the receipt?", says "The request can be paid without one. This is recorded." Add a note if you like, then select "Waive receipt" again.

## Correct a mistake

- To undo a payment, select "Reverse payment" and give a reason. The request returns to "Approved, waiting for payment", and both entries stay on record. The history shows "Payment corrected".
- To send a request back to the requester, select "Send back to requester".
- To close a request so it cannot be paid, select "Void request" and give a reason. Nothing is deleted. The requester sees that the request was closed.
- "Add a note" adds a note that only the treasury team can see.

## Approve as treasurer

When no one in the group is able to approve a request, it appears under "Needs treasurer approval". Select "Approve as treasurer". This needs a second person who can record payments, because whoever approves cannot also pay. If there is no second person, the page says "This needs a second person who can record payments before you can approve it." and "Add one under Treasury team."

## Set Reimbursements up

The links "Reimbursement settings" and "Treasury team" are at the top of the "Reimbursement requests" page for people who manage reimbursements.

On "Reimbursement settings":

- Under "Groups that can receive requests", switch on "Can receive requests" for each group people may charge. Each group shows how many chairs or leaders can approve. If a group has none, the page says requests go to the treasurer. Until you choose at least one group, the page says "No group can receive requests yet. Choose at least one so people can make requests."
- Under "Categories", use "Add a category" to name the categories people choose from. "Rename" changes a name. "Retire" removes a category from the list people choose from. Retired categories stay on old requests.

On "Treasury team":

- "Who has a treasury role" lists the people who hold a treasury role and what each can do, such as "View all requests", "Record payments" and "Manage reimbursements".
- Under "Roles to set up", "Set up the Treasurer role" and "Set up the Reimbursement Clerk role" make those roles available. A Reimbursement Clerk can enter requests on behalf of members who don't use the portal.
- "Give a role" gives a role to a person. "End this role" takes it away.

You can only give permissions you hold yourself.

## If no one can record payments

If your church has no treasurer yet, the queue says "No one can record payments yet. Your church office can ask PresbyPortal support to set up a treasurer." The PresbyPortal team designates a congregation's first treasurer by invitation. See [Accepting your invitation](/help/accepting-your-invitation). {{contact}}

## Turning Reimbursements off

An administrator can turn Reimbursements off under "Features". That hides every reimbursement page and deletes nothing.
