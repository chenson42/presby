Where "Per-Capita, SASR & Imports" is turned on, a presbytery can read six reports about its member congregations. Each report is a page on screen and a file you can download and open in a spreadsheet. The reports cover the congregations that are members of your presbytery today. New worshiping communities are not included, and neither is a congregation that has left.

## Open the reports

Open "Administration", then choose the "Per-Capita, SASR & Imports" tile under "Reports & Insights". The page opens with a section headed "Reports and exports", with one card for each report. Choose a card to open the report.

Each report needs a permission, and having one does not open a report that needs a different one. A card you cannot open says "Locked" and what it needs. An administrator can give the permission under "Roles". See [Roles: who can do what](/help/roles-who-can-do-what).

| Report | What it shows | Where its data is entered | Permission you need |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Vacant pulpits" | Congregations with no installed or designated pastor today | Pastoral appointments, on "Credentials" | Manage credentials |
| "Membership trend" | Ending active members by congregation and year | Statistics, in the Congregation Statistics section of "Per-Capita, SASR & Imports", or reported by the congregation | Manage statistics |
| "Viability" | How the presbytery rates each congregation | "Congregation Oversight" | Manage congregation oversight |
| "Per capita" | What each congregation was billed for a year, has paid and still owes | The rate and the bills in the Per-Capita section of "Per-Capita, SASR & Imports" | Manage per-capita billing |
| "Buildings" | Every building in use for each congregation, with its year, size, condition and safety systems | "Congregation Oversight" | Manage congregation oversight |
| "Insurance and loans" | Each congregation's newest insurance, its open loans and its appraised value | "Congregation Oversight" | Manage congregation property finances |

## Insights and analytics

On your presbytery's home page, choose "Insights & Analytics" under "Reports & Insights". The page is a summary of your member congregations. It is read-only: nothing on it can be changed, and it has no download.

Each card comes from one report and needs that report's permission. A card you cannot open says "Locked" and what it needs. If you have none of the four permissions, the page says you don't have permission and names them. An administrator can give a permission under "Roles". See [Roles: who can do what](/help/roles-who-can-do-what).

| Card | What it shows | Permission you need |
|---|---|---|
| "Member congregations" | How many congregations are members of your presbytery today | Any one of the four permissions in this table |
| "Ending active members" | The total for the newest of the five years ending last year that has statistics, and how many congregations reported | Manage statistics |
| "Vacant pulpits" | How many congregations have no installed or designated pastor today | Manage credentials |
| "Per capita" | What was billed, paid and still owed for this year | Manage per-capita billing |
| "Viability" | Every member congregation with the rating the presbytery recorded, at risk first | Manage congregation oversight |

The "Ending active members" and "Per capita" cards name the year they show. "Member congregations", "Vacant pulpits" and "Viability" show how things stand today. When nothing is recorded, the card says so in words and does not show a zero. "Ending active members" says how many congregations reported, and its total leaves out any that did not report a figure. "Vacant pulpits" has the same limit as the report: a congregation whose appointment was never recorded counts as vacant. "Per capita" shows the current year's totals, which include a congregation that has left but still has a bill.

A card opens its report only where the reports are turned on, and a congregation's name opens its page on "Congregation Oversight" only where that is turned on. Otherwise the figure or name shows without a link. A card that says "We couldn't load this card" has a "Try again" link; the other cards are not affected.

If the page says Insights & Analytics "isn't turned on" for your presbytery, the PresbyPortal team switches it on for the whole platform. [Contact the team](/help/contact) if you need it.

## The viability map

Above the viability list, a section headed "Congregation map" shows your member congregations as pins. You need the permission to manage congregation oversight to see it. Without that permission the Viability section says "Locked" and there is no map.

Each pin is a member congregation that has a location, and its shape shows its rating: a diamond for "At risk", a square for "Fair", a circle for "Healthy" and a hollow circle for "Not yet assessed". The key under the map lists the shapes in words. A line below the map says how many congregations have a location, for example "2 of 12 congregations have a location."

Choose a pin to see the congregation's name and rating. Where "Congregation Oversight" is turned on, the pin also has an "Open oversight page" link. On a computer, drag the map to move it and use the + and - buttons to zoom. On a phone or tablet, one finger scrolls the page past the map; use two fingers to move or zoom the map, or use the + and - buttons. The viability list below the map names every congregation with the same ratings, so you can read the page without the map.

Congregations with no usable location are listed under "Not on the map". If none has a location, the section says so instead of showing a map. If the map cannot load, the section says "The map could not load", or "We couldn't load the map" with a "Try again" link, and the list below still works. Reloading the page tries again.

The map pictures come from MapTiler and are credited to MapTiler and OpenStreetMap contributors under the map. Your browser asks MapTiler for them directly while the map is showing. See the [Privacy policy](/help/privacy-policy).

If you can open the Viability section, it shows no error, and there is no map section above it, the map is not switched on for PresbyPortal. [Contact the team](/help/contact) if you need it.

## Vacant pulpits

A congregation is listed when it has no installed pastor and no designated pastor recorded as serving today. Stated supply, interim pastor and temporary supply do not fill the pulpit here; the most recent one shows under "Supply". A parish associate does not fill the pulpit either, and does not make it vacant. A call that starts on a later date does not fill the pulpit until that date, and a call with an end date still fills it until that day.

"Vacant since" is the end date of the latest installed or designated call on record. If none has ended, it says "No earlier pastor on record". The report shows call types and dates, never a person's name.

The list is built from the pastoral appointments recorded on "Credentials" (under "Pastoral appointments"), so a congregation whose appointment was never recorded appears as vacant. If your presbytery has recorded none for its congregations, the page says "No pastoral appointments are recorded yet" and does not list anything.

## Membership trend

The grid shows ending active members for each congregation, five years at a time. Use "Earlier years" and "Later years" to move. Each cell is a number, or one of three words:

- A dash: the congregation was not a member of your presbytery that year.
- "No data": it was a member and no statistics are on file for that year.
- "Not reported": statistics are on file but have no figure for ending active members.

Under each year, "Total reported" adds the numbers shown and says how many congregations reported, for example "9 of 12 reporting". Where a congregation reported its own figure, that figure is used. To add a year, use "Record statistics" in the Congregation Statistics section.

## Viability

Each congregation shows the rating the presbytery recorded on "Congregation Oversight": "At risk", "Fair" or "Healthy", with the redevelopment notes and the date it was last updated. A congregation with no rating says "Not yet assessed". The scale is 1 for at risk and 3 for healthy. A file from an older system may use the opposite scale, so check before comparing the two.

## Putting a congregation on the map

A congregation appears on the map when it has a location on "Congregation Oversight". "Congregation Oversight" must be turned on (the PresbyPortal team switches it on for the whole platform), and you need the permission to manage congregation oversight.

1. Open "Congregation Oversight", then choose "Open" beside the congregation.
2. In the "Assessment" section, choose "Edit assessment".
3. Under "Map location", enter the "Latitude" first. It is a decimal number between -90 and 90, for example 39.9612.
4. Enter the "Longitude". It is a decimal number between -180 and 180. A congregation in the United States has a negative longitude, for example -82.9988.
5. Choose "Save".

Enter both numbers or neither. To copy them from a maps application, copy the pair of numbers it shows, for example 39.9612, -82.9988, and paste it into either box: both boxes fill themselves, and you should check them before you save. A latitude and longitude of 0 and 0 is refused, because it is a point in the ocean. If a pin lands in the wrong place, check the signs. To take a congregation off the map, clear both boxes and choose "Save".

Where the map is turned on, open "Insights & Analytics" after you save to see the pin. A congregation that has left your presbytery or closed is not on the map, even with a location.

## Per capita

Choose a year with the arrows. Each row shows what the congregation was billed, what it has paid and what is still owed, with a total at the bottom. A congregation that paid more than it was billed shows its still-owed amount as a credit. A congregation with no bill yet shows "Not billed" and says why: either there are no statistics on file for the year the bill is based on, or the bill has not been generated yet. A congregation that has left but still has a bill is kept and marked "No longer a member". Amounts are rounded to the nearest cent. "Export all years" downloads every bill the presbytery has recorded, for every year.

## Buildings

The "Buildings" report lists each member congregation's buildings that are in use, from the records on "Congregation Oversight". You need the permission to manage congregation oversight. Each building shows its type, the year built, the square feet, the condition of the roof, plumbing, heating and cooling (HVAC) and boiler, whether it has sprinklers, a fire alarm and a security system, and whether it is the "Primary" building. A figure that was never recorded says "Not recorded". Above the list, a line says how many buildings are listed and how many congregations they belong to.

A building that was retired is not listed, because it is no longer in use. A congregation with no building in use still has one line. It says "None in use" when every building recorded for it has been retired, and "Not recorded" when no building was ever recorded. A congregation that is no longer active is not listed.

"Export CSV" downloads one row for each building, or one row for a congregation with none. The file carries the congregation, its city and county, and the facts above. It leaves out street addresses and notes.

## Insurance and loans

The "Insurance and loans" report has one line for each member congregation, from the records on "Congregation Oversight". You need the permission to manage congregation property finances, which is not the permission for "Buildings". Anyone who holds it is asked to set up two-factor sign-in the next time they sign in, if they have not already. See [Setting up two-factor sign-in](/help/setting-up-two-factor).

Each line shows the congregation's newest insurance year: the "Carrier", the "Policy year", when the policy expires, whether it had recent claims and whether it is interested in the insurance pool. A policy whose expiry date has passed says "Expired" in words. It then shows the congregation's open loans: how many, the original amount, the current balance, any grants, and the "Guarantee" written on each loan. Last, it shows the appraised value, which adds up each building in use's newest appraisal. A loan that has been paid off is not counted, and neither is a building that was retired. A "Total" line closes the list.

A figure nobody recorded says "Not recorded" and is never shown as $0. Where a total leaves some figures out, it says how many, for example "1 not recorded". A congregation with no insurance or loans recorded still has a line.

"Export CSV" downloads one row for each congregation. It carries the figures above, and it includes each loan's guarantee. It leaves out street addresses, policy numbers, lenders and notes. A figure that was not recorded is an empty cell, and two extra columns count what is missing: the open loans with no balance, and the buildings with no appraisal.

## Downloading a report

Choose "Export CSV". The file is the table as shown: one row for each congregation, for each building in "Buildings", or for each congregation and year in the membership trend. Each of the files for vacant pulpits, membership trend, viability and per capita holds no email addresses, street addresses, coordinates or people's names, apart from anything written in the redevelopment notes. What the two files from "Congregation Oversight" leave out is under their own headings above. If a text cell begins with =, +, - or @, the file adds a leading apostrophe so a spreadsheet shows it as text and does not run it as a formula. The file is UTF-8 with a byte-order mark, so Excel shows accented letters correctly. Every export is recorded in the audit trail with which report, who exported it and how many rows, and never the names or notes in it.

## If a report is locked or missing

- A card that says "Locked": you do not have the permission it needs. Ask an administrator to give it under "Roles".
- A page that says it "isn't turned on" for your presbytery: the reports are switched on by the PresbyPortal team for the whole platform, not by an administrator. [Contact the team](/help/contact) if you need them.
- A page that says it "isn't available": reports are for presbyteries only.

More on missing access is in [Why can't I see this?](/help/why-cant-i-see-this).
