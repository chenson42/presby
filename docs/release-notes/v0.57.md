## 0.57.0 — 2026-11-15

### Feature: Presbytery reports and downloads

**Value:** A presbytery's staff can now see, in one place, which congregations have no pastor, how membership has moved over the years, which congregations are at risk, and who owes per capita, and take each one away as a spreadsheet file for a meeting. Every figure comes from what the presbytery already records; nothing new has to be entered.

#### What's New

- **A "Reports and exports" section** on the presbytery's Per-Capita, SASR & Imports page (under Administration), with four reports. A report the viewer may not open is shown "Locked" with the reason, not hidden.
- **Vacant pulpits.** Member congregations with no installed or designated pastor today, longest vacant first, with how long and since when, and any interim or stated supply. Supply does not count as filling the pulpit, so this count may be higher than an older list that counted it. If no appointments have been recorded at all, the page says so instead of listing every congregation as vacant.
- **Membership trend.** Ending active members by congregation and year, five years at a time (up to ten), with how many congregations reported each year. A dash means the congregation was not a member that year; "No data" means it was a member but nothing was recorded.
- **Viability.** Every member congregation, at risk first, with its score on PresbyPortal's scale (1 = at risk, 3 = healthy), notes and when it was last updated.
- **Per capita.** Billed, paid and still owed by congregation for a year, with totals; an overpayment shows as a credit; congregations not billed say why; a congregation that has left but still owes stays on the list. "Export all years" downloads every year at once.
- **CSV downloads.** Each report downloads as the rows shown, ready for Excel (accented letters display correctly). The files hold no email addresses, street addresses or coordinates, and no names apart from anything written in the viability notes. Every download is recorded in the audit trail with who, which report and how many rows.
- Reports and pages work on a phone: each congregation becomes a card.

#### Permissions

No new permission. Each report uses the key that already covers its data:

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `credentials.manage` | Vacant pulpits |
| `statistics.manage` | Membership trend |
| `congregation_oversight.manage` | Viability |
| `per_capita.manage` | Per capita |

A presbytery's founding administrator holds all four. The reports appear where the platform's Reports setting is on.

#### New Routes

- `GET /o/<slug>/admin/reports/vacant-pulpits` (and `/export`)
- `GET /o/<slug>/admin/reports/membership-trend` (and `/export`)
- `GET /o/<slug>/admin/reports/viability` (and `/export`)
- `GET /o/<slug>/admin/reports/per-capita` (and `/export`)

#### Also fixed

- A mistyped congregation address under Reports now shows "page not found" instead of an error message.
- A malformed year in the Reports page's address no longer jumps to the year 1900.

**Help:** /help/presbytery-reports (new).
