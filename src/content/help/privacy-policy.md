PresbyPortal is a service that helps Presbyterian congregations, presbyteries and synods keep their records and do their work. This page says what information we hold, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and what you can ask us to do. In this policy, "we" means PresbyPortal.

## Who is responsible for what

There are two kinds of information in PresbyPortal.

- **Account information** is about you as a person who signs in. We decide how it is used.
- **Congregation records** are the people, households, rolls and other records that a congregation, presbytery or synod enters. That organization decides what to keep and who may see it. We handle these records on its behalf, to run the service for it.

## Account information we keep

If you have an account, we keep:

- your name and email address
- a hashed password, if you sign in with a password. We do not keep the password itself
- if you use two-factor sign-in, the secret for your authenticator app, stored encrypted, and your recovery codes, stored as hashes
- when you last signed in, and how many sign-in attempts in a row have failed, so we can lock an account that someone is trying to guess into
- your account roles, which say what you may do

## If you sign in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google sends us your name, your email address, the web address of your profile picture, your Google account ID, and whether Google has verified your email address. We refuse the sign-in if Google has not verified it. We ask Google for nothing more than your basic profile, so we cannot read your Google mail, contacts, calendar or files.

We keep your name, email address, profile picture address and Google account ID, along with the sign-in tokens Google sends back. We use them to sign you in and to protect your account, and for nothing else. We do not show your Google picture anywhere in PresbyPortal.

## Records a congregation keeps

An organization using PresbyPortal may keep:

- people and households, with their contact details and dates such as birth dates
- the membership roll and the actions recorded on it
- officers and their terms, and groups
- each person's privacy settings
- events and calendars
- giving records, if the congregation uses giving
- children's ministry rosters, with guardians and emergency contacts
- job openings and the content of its public website
- other records the organization chooses to keep

The organization decides what goes in, who can see it, and what stays on the roll. Messages sent through a congregation's website contact form go to that congregation.

## Other information we record

- **Audit records.** When something important happens, such as a role change, a feature turned on or off, a change to a record, or an account locked after failed sign-ins, we record what happened, who did it and when, along with the IP address and browser type of the request.
- **Abuse protection.** To slow down guessing and misuse, we count requests by IP address, and on sign-in forms by the email address typed, for a short time.
- **Email.** We send emails such as invitations, password resets and email-change confirmations. We keep a copy of each one (who it went to, its subject and its content) and its status: sent, failed, delivered, bounced or reported as spam. If our email service reports it, we also learn whether an email was opened or a link in it was clicked.
- **Messages to us.** Feedback and tickets you send. Feedback sent from inside a congregation goes first to a person at that congregation.

## Cookies

We use the cookies needed to sign you in and keep you signed in, and a short-lived one for a single step: accepting an invitation. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell or rent personal information.
- We do not show advertising.
- We do not use analytics or tracking tools on PresbyPortal's pages.

## Other companies that handle information

We use these companies to run PresbyPortal, and share information with them only for that purpose.

- **Vercel** hosts the website and runs the service.
- **Neon** hosts our database in the United States. Account information, congregation records, audit records and the log of emails are stored there.
- **Resend** sends our emails, so it handles the address and content of each one.
- **Stripe** takes online gifts for congregations that use giving. You leave PresbyPortal for a page hosted by Stripe, and Stripe's own policy applies there. We do not receive or store your card number.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** checks that a person, not a bot, is using a form, where we turn it on. Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser details when it does.
- **Upstash** keeps the short-lived request counts for abuse protection, where we use it.
- **Google** handles your sign-in, only if you choose Google.

Our hosting and database providers also keep ordinary technical logs. We share information with no one else unless the law requires it.

## How long we keep information

- **Congregation records are permanent by design.** People are never deleted from PresbyPortal. A record is corrected, merged with a duplicate, or marked as ended, not erased.
- **Accounts** are kept until you ask us to deactivate yours. A deactivated account is kept, and stays blocked from signing in.
- **Audit records and the log of emails sent** are kept. They are not deleted on a schedule.
- **Password-reset and email-change links** expire, and expired ones are cleared each day.

## What you can ask us to do

You can ask us to:

- tell you what account information we hold about you
- correct it. You can also change your name, email address and password yourself in "Account settings" (see [Your account](/help/your-account))
- deactivate your account, so no one can sign in with it

"Delete account" in "Account settings" records that you asked, but it does not remove anything on its own. Ask us instead, and say what you want. We tell you what we can do.

Records a congregation keeps about you, such as your place on its roll, its directory or its giving records, belong to that congregation. Ask the congregation to show you, correct or limit them. We do not erase them on request, because the roll is a permanent record.

## Security

We protect your information with two-factor sign-in, hashed passwords, encrypted connections, and database rules that keep each congregation's records apart from every other congregation's. No system is completely secure, and we cannot promise otherwise. If you think your account is at risk, tell us right away.

## Children

PresbyPortal accounts are for adults, and we do not knowingly create an account for a child. A congregation may keep records about children, such as a children's ministry roster with guardians. Those are the congregation's records, and the congregation decides who sees them. If you think a child has an account, tell us.
