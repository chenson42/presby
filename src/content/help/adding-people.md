Community is where you add people and record how each one is connected to your congregation: as a visitor, a participant or a member. A new member waits for the Session to approve. You can also mark how a person is doing, such as inactive, and archive a visitor you no longer need on a list.

## Turn on Community

1. Open "Administration", then "Features". See [Turning features on and off](/help/turning-features-on-and-off).
2. Turn on "Community: add people and record roll actions".

Until you do, the "Community" tile opens a page that says "Member management isn't turned on" for your congregation.

## The Community page

The page is headed "Community". It has two links at the top, "Pending approvals" and "Add person", and a row of views: "Everyone", "Members", "Participants", "Visitors", "Inactive", "Staff" and "Former". There is a "Search people" box.

![The Community page on a phone: the view tabs, a search box, and a card for each person with an Edit button.](shot:community-list)

- "Visitors" lists the people you recorded as visitors. A visitor is never in the directory.
- "Inactive" lists people you marked inactive or moved away who are not on the roll. People on the roll stay under "Members" or "Participants", with a small "Inactive" or "Moved away" tag.
- "Staff" lists people with a staff position, and a person with no position yet is marked "No position yet".
- "Former" lists people whose connection has ended, with the date: participants you removed, and visitors or inactive people you archived.

## Adding a person

1. Choose "Add person". The form has steps, shown as "Step 1 of 6".
2. Find a matching record. Enter a first name and a last name (both required), and a date of birth or an email address if you have one. Choose "Search". This is so you do not create the same person twice.
3. If someone matches, the next step is "Confirm the match". It says, for example, "C. Wainwright is already in this community" and what they are. Choose "Open their record", or "No, this is someone new" to carry on.
4. Name and birth date. Then contact and address, then household ("No household yet", "Add to an existing household" or "Create a new household").
5. Connection. Choose how this person is connected. The next section describes each choice.
6. Review the summary and choose "Add person".

A record is not a sign-in. To let the person sign in and see your congregation's pages, invite them from their record. See [Inviting people to sign in](/help/sign-in-invitations).

On the contact step, a person can have more than one email address. One is marked "Main address", and the directory shows it unless the person hides their email. "Add another email address" adds a row. Each row can be labelled Personal, Work or Other, and "Don't send mail to this address" keeps routine mail away from that address.

### The three connections

- "Visitor": someone who has visited. Not on any roll, and not listed in the directory. You can add the date of a first visit and how they heard about you. No approval is needed, and the message is "Visitor added."
- "Participant (takes effect now)": takes part in the life of the congregation but is not a member. Counted as an other participant from the effective date, which you choose. No approval is needed, and the message is "Participant added."
- "Member: profession of faith (needs Session approval)": received by profession of faith. They count as a member once the Session approves. See "Pending approvals" below.

For a participant or a member you enter an effective date, and you can add a minute reference.

## Changing a connection later

Open "Edit" beside a person in a list. The "Connection" section shows where they stand.

- A participant has "Remove as participant" and "Undo last entry". Removing asks for an effective date and ends their connection from that date. They leave the directory and lose access if they have an account. Undo asks for a reason, keeps the original entry in the record marked undone, and puts the person back as they were.
- "Record a roll action" offers "Profession of faith", "Reaffirmation of faith", "Restoration", "Certificate received (transfer in)", "Other gain", "Enrolled as a baptized member", "Enrolled as an affiliate member" and "Enrolled as a participant". Enrolling someone as a participant takes effect now. Every other action is proposed and waits for the Session to approve it.

## Marking how a person is doing

Open "Edit" beside a person. The "Engagement" card says how the person is doing: "Visitor", "Regular attender", "Inactive" or "Moved away". It is for your own care. It is never reported to the presbytery or the denomination, and it does not change anyone's membership roll. It does decide who appears in the member directory and what a connected sign-in can see.

A person who is not on the roll appears in the directory only as a regular attender. A person on the roll stays in the directory whatever you choose, and cannot be marked as a visitor; "Moved away" is a label, and a departure is recorded as a roll action. If a person on the roll is still marked as a visitor, the card offers "Mark as regular attender". A staff record has no engagement: the card says "This is a staff record, so engagement isn't tracked."

When a change would add someone to the directory or take them out, a dialog asks first. It adds a line if the person has a sign-in connected, and, when it applies, a line saying they could be connected to the record automatically when they sign in with an address on file. Choose "Save engagement". Every change is recorded. On the "Inactive" list, "Mark as regular" does the same from the row.

## Archiving a visitor, and restoring

A visitor, or a person marked inactive or moved away, can be archived with "Archive", from their row or their record. The dialog says they will leave the list and appear under "Former", that their record is kept, and that you can restore it any time. It adds a line if they have a sign-in connected, and another if an invitation to them is still open.

Someone who still holds an office, a role or a staff position cannot be archived: end that first. A member on the roll leaves through a roll action, and a participant is removed as a participant. You cannot archive your own record.

To bring someone back, open "Former" and choose "Restore", or choose "Restore" on their record. A dialog asks first, with "Restore" and "Keep archived". When you add a person who was archived, the match step says so and offers the same button.

## Correcting a visit

On a visitor's record, "Visit details" lets you correct the date of the first visit and how they heard about you. Choose "Save visit details".

## Pending approvals

"Pending approvals" lists member proposals that wait for the Session. A count beside the link, such as "1 waiting", says how many are waiting.

If you are a member of the Session and your sign-in is connected to your record, you can decide each proposal.

- "Approve" records the action on the roll from its effective date. You can add a minute reference.
- "Deny" asks for a reason, up to 500 characters, and the action is not recorded.
- "Select all on this page" lets you approve up to 50 at a time.
- When the person who approves also proposed the entry, "Recently decided" marks it "Self-approved".

If you proposed an entry, you see "You proposed this" and "Withdraw". Withdrawing asks for a reason, keeps the proposal in the record marked withdrawn, and lets you propose again. "Recently decided" shows what the Session has decided, with the reasons.

A member of the Session approves only after their sign-in is connected to their record. See [Inviting people to sign in](/help/sign-in-invitations).

### What the banner means

When no one can approve, a banner at the top of "Pending approvals" says why. When someone can approve, there is no banner.

- "Your Session roster is empty": no Session member is recorded. Record your ruling elders, installed pastors and associate pastors under "Officers". See [Officers and the Session](/help/officers-and-the-session).
- "No one can approve yet", and the banner says the term has not begun: a Session member is recorded, but the term starts on a later date, which the banner gives. The date decides. The banner adds that, to approve, a member of the Session also needs to have connected a sign-in.
- "No one can approve yet", with "none has connected a sign-in yet": Session members are recorded, but none has connected a sign-in to their record. If you can manage both people and officers, the banner lists each Session member and where their invitation stands, for example "Not invited yet" with an "Invite" link. Invite them from there. See [Inviting people to sign in](/help/sign-in-invitations). Anyone else sees "Ask an administrator to invite your Session members to sign in."
- "No one can approve yet", with "none of them has permission to approve roll actions": Session members have connected a sign-in, but none can approve. This usually means the Session member role was changed. Check "Roles", or send a support ticket.
