## 0.39.2 — 2026-10-23

### Fix: founding administrators and treasurers now appear in Community

**Value:** Someone who joined a congregation by accepting a founding-administrator or treasurer invitation was connected to it, and search found them, but none of Community's six lists showed them. They now appear in **Staff**. If they hold no staff position yet, the row says "No position yet" and, for people who manage staff, links to the Staff page to add one. The member Directory and the Everyone list are unchanged.

### Fix: the "not connected yet" page tells the truth

**Value:** When you sign in with an account that no congregation has connected, the page used to say that someone at your church could add you using your email address. That isn't how it works. An address on a person's record never connects an account; only accepting an invitation does. The page now says exactly that, shows the address you are signed in with, and calls the product PresbyPortal.

**Technical:** `src/lib/community.ts` (the Staff view also lists live memberships with engagement `staff`); `src/app/no-organization/page.tsx`. Includes a regression test that every kind of live membership lands in at least one Community view. Work-log: `docs/work-log/2026-10-23-community-staff-anchor.md`.

## 0.39.0 — 2026-10-23

### Feature: Community replaces Members, with visitors, participants and member proposals

**Value:** A congregation's first administrator could open the Members page and find "forbidden" with no Add person button, and even a clerk had no way to record someone who is not on the member roll. Community fixes that page and gives a new congregation a way to start recording the people around it. **It does not yet let anyone approve a member. That arrives in the next release.**

**Read this first: what this release cannot do yet.**

- **Members can be proposed, but nobody can approve them yet.** You can add a person as a member (profession of faith, reaffirmation, and so on). That entry then waits for your Session, and in this release no role can approve it and there is no way to withdraw it. Approval and withdrawal arrive in the next release.
- **A waiting proposal blocks enrolling or removing that person as a participant until then.** If you only need someone in your records for now, add them as a visitor or a participant instead. Visitors and participants take effect straight away and are not affected.
- **Undo covers participant entries only.** It cannot undo a member proposal or an approved member change.

**What's new**

- **Community replaces Members.** The administration section is now called Community, at a new address. Old Members links and bookmarks redirect to it. The Directory that members see keeps its name, and so does the wording of your statistical reports. Nothing here changes a return you have already filed: if a change reaches a year you have filed, the page warns you, and a correcting return is the only way to correct it.
- **The page no longer dead-ends.** Someone who can manage people but cannot view the directory now sees the page, with Add person and the Visitors, Staff and Former lists, plus a note naming the missing permission. Someone who cannot manage people sees the same forbidden page as before.
- **Six views.** Everyone, Members, Participants, Visitors, Staff and Former. "Everyone" is the directory list, so it does not include visitors or staff; they have their own views. Staff is anyone with an open staff position.
- **Visitors.** Add someone who has come to worship, with their first-visit date and how they heard of you. This needs only permission to manage people. A visitor does not appear in the member Directory.
- **Participants are recorded on the spot.** Someone who takes part in the life of the congregation without being on the member roll can be enrolled, and later removed, without waiting for the Session. This needs permission to propose roll actions. The stated clerk role now includes it, and congregations whose first administrator was designated through the platform have it added to the clerk role that administrator holds. Dates cannot be in the future. Participants count in your participant and Total Adherents statistics from the date you enter.
- **Removing a participant** ends their connection to the congregation from that date. They leave the directory and lose access if they have an account. A person who holds an office, an open staff position or a role cannot be removed until that ends.
- **Undo.** The latest participant enrolment or removal for a person can be undone, with a required reason. Undo can be refused, for example if something about the person has changed since. A removal that is undone reopens the connection.
- **"Already here."** When you add someone who is already at your congregation, the match step says so and links to their record, instead of the old message about another organization.
- **Fixed: a new member's roll status.** A person added through Add person could be left with a blank current roll status, and every profession of faith was read as "18 and older". Both are now filled in correctly.
- **Everything is recorded.** Each participant entry and each undo is written to the audit log; an undo records its reason. These entries are recorded separately from Session approvals, so a participant entry never looks like an approved one.

**One limit to know about.** Visitors are not in the member Directory, but they are in the same automatic group as every current member, and that group carries the baseline member permissions. Today only invitation acceptance connects a sign-in to a person, so this exposes nothing now. It will be closed before congregations can invite people to sign in.

**Settings.** The per-congregation switch for this section is now named "Community: add people and record roll actions" (it was "Add & approve members"). It is still off until you turn it on. Removing and undoing also need the existing roll-action editing setting to be on.

**For the operator: deploy order.** Deploy the code first, then apply migration `0061`. If `0061` is applied first, Add person fails until the code is live, because the new database rule refuses what the old code writes. `0061` is safe to apply more than once.

**Technical:** migration `0061_presby_participant_roll` (the `roll.propose` and `roll.approve` catalog keys, the clerk template bindings, three validated checks and one index on `roll_actions`, a restrictive insert policy, and `presby_record_participant_action()` and `presby_void_participant_action()`); DECISION-197–201; findings F172–F178 (`docs/schema-design-2.md` §2u). The approval step itself is still enforced by the application, not the database, until the next release. Work-log: `docs/work-log/2026-10-22-founding-admin-directory.md`.
