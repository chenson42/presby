There are four ways to get help: ask the person who invited you, write to the PresbyPortal team, send feedback, or file a ticket. This page says what each one is for and who reads it.

## Ask the person who invited you

For a question about your own congregation, the person who invited you or your church administrator is the right person to ask. They can see your congregation's settings and people.

## Write to the PresbyPortal team

You do not need an account. Open the [contact form](/help/contact). Under "What is this about?" choose a topic, give your email address under "Your email address", write your "Your message" and select "Send message". The page says "Your message was sent."

Use it when you cannot sign in, when you have no one to ask, or when you want to ask about starting a congregation. A person on the PresbyPortal team reads each message. There is no automatic reply and no promised time. If we reply, it is by email from a person. Do not put passwords or recovery codes in a message.

A message is not proof of who you are. The team does not change anyone's account, role or sign-in because of a message alone. For anything about an account, we reply to the email address on that account, which may not be the one you typed in the form.

This is not a ticket. A ticket is for a signed-in church administrator, has a reply thread and belongs to your congregation. A contact message is one note to the team and belongs to no congregation.

## Send feedback

There are two places to send feedback, and they go to different people.

**Feedback for the PresbyPortal team.** Use the card on your home page that asks "How are we doing?" and choose "Share feedback". Or open "Account settings" and use "Send feedback". Choose a category if you like (Suggestion, Bug report or General feedback), write your message and send it. A message can be up to 2000 characters. The page says "Thanks — we read every one." and the PresbyPortal team is told.

On your home page, "Not today" hides the card for the rest of the day. "Stop asking" turns the daily card off after you confirm, and "Re-enable prompt" in "Account settings" turns it back on.

**Feedback for your congregation.** Inside a congregation, open the account menu and choose "Give feedback". The page says "A designated role-holder reviews everything shared here." That means the feedback goes to someone at your congregation, not to the PresbyPortal team. They can turn it into a ticket for the PresbyPortal team with "Promote to ticket", or "Dismiss" it.

## File a ticket

A ticket is a message to the PresbyPortal team about a problem or a change your congregation needs. Tickets are for church administrators. A person whose role allows it, such as the founding administrator, can file and manage them. Anyone else who opens "Tickets" sees "Restricted" and is pointed to the feedback form.

1. Open "Administration", then choose "Tickets".
2. Choose "File a ticket".
3. Fill in "Subject" and "Description".
4. Choose a "Category" (Content, Configuration, Theme, Bug or Feature request), an "Area" (Directory, Roll, Roles, Giving, Events, Website, Account or Other) and a "Priority" (Low, Normal, High or Urgent).
5. If it helps, add an attachment: a PNG, JPEG, WEBP or PDF file up to 10MB.
6. Select "File ticket".

The page says "Ticket filed." and opens the ticket, which starts with the status "New". You get an email that confirms it. On the ticket you can write a reply with "Send reply".

## What to expect

The PresbyPortal team is told about every new ticket and every piece of feedback it receives. A contact message is read when someone on the team opens the inbox. There is no promised reply time for tickets, feedback or contact messages.
