The calendar shows your congregation's events to its members. Some events can also appear on your public website. This page covers reading the calendar, adding events, repeating events, event types, and what the public calendar needs.

## The calendar every member sees

Choose the "Calendar" tile under "Worship & Events". The page is headed "Calendar". On a phone it shows a list of days. The links "Month" and "List" switch the view, and the arrows move between months. "Show filters" lets you filter by event type. "Copy month" copies the month's events so you can paste them into a bulletin, and leaves out events that are not open to everyone. Choose an event to read its details. A cancelled event is not shown.

![The Calendar page on a phone in list view: the month, then each day with its events.](shot:calendar-month)

Your congregation's page also has a "Coming up" card with the next events.

The calendar is on from the start. If it is off, you can turn it on under "Features": it is called "Member calendar".

## Who can add events

Reading the calendar comes with the Member role. Adding and changing events needs a role that allows "Create, edit, and cancel calendar events, including repeating series". A Founding Administrator does not hold that role, and cannot create it or give it, because a role can include only what you hold. See [Roles: who can do what](/help/roles-who-can-do-what).

On the first day, "Manage events" says "You don't have permission to manage events". The PresbyPortal team sets this up. {{contactAdmin}}

When someone holds the role, the "Calendar" page shows "Add an event" and "Manage events" at the top. The rest of this page describes what they see.

## Adding an event

1. Choose "Manage events", then "New event".
2. Enter a title and the start time. A description, a location, an end time and an event type are optional.
3. Choose "Create event". The message is "Event created."

The events list shows each event with its time and status. Choose an event to edit it with "Edit event", or choose "Cancel event". The dialog says the event stays on record and is not deleted. A cancelled event stays in the events list, marked "Cancelled", and leaves the calendar.

### Open to everyone, or members only

"Open to everyone, not only members" is ticked at first. If you untick it, the event shows only on the member calendar, for members who have calendar access. It also stays out of "Copy month" and cannot be shown on the website.

## A repeating event

Tick "This event repeats" on the form.

- "A simple interval" repeats "Weekly", "Every 2 weeks" or "Monthly (same date)".
- "A specific day of the month" repeats on, for example, the first Sunday. You choose "Which" (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th or last) and the day of the week.
- "Number of occurrences" is a number from 1 to 52. Anything else says "Enter a number of occurrences from 1 to 52."

Each date becomes its own event, and each is marked "Part of a series" on its page. To add more dates, use "Extend this series". It adds dates going forward, and the events already on the calendar are never moved or rewritten.

## Event types

An event type colours the event on the calendar and gives it an icon. Choose "Event types" on the events list.

- A new congregation has a set of types. Some are switched off at first and marked "Retired". Each type has an "Offered" switch.
- A retired type stays on the events that already use it. It is not offered for new events and is not a filter.
- "General" is the default type. The page says "The default type can't be retired."
- To add one, use "Add an event type": a name, a colour and an icon, then "Add event type". The arrows beside a type change its order.

Types need a role too: one that allows "Create, rename, recolor, reorder and retire the event types that color-code the calendar". The page says "You don't have permission to manage event types". A Founding Administrator does not hold it at first.

## Showing events on your public website

The public calendar shows chosen events on your congregation's website. Three things all have to be true before an event appears there.

1. "Public events calendar" is turned on under "Features". You can do this yourself. It is off at first.
2. The event is marked for the website. On the event form, tick "Show on our website's public calendar". It is off by default, and it cannot be ticked on an event that is members only. The form warns that anyone on the internet can read the event's title, description, location and event type, and that search engines and web archives may keep copies, so you should leave out private details.
3. Whoever maintains your website has put the calendar on a page. This is not something you do in PresbyPortal.

If the first two are done and nothing shows on your website, the third is missing. Ask whoever maintains your website to put the calendar on a page. The event's own page says the same: it appears once your public events calendar is turned on and your website has a calendar page.

To mark a whole series at once, open any event in the series and use "Put this and later events on the website". A dialog says how many events it marks, and a message confirms it. "Take this and later events off the website" takes them off. Taking an event off later does not remove copies that search engines or archives already made.

The public calendar shows one month at a time with "Previous", "Today" and "Next". A cancelled event is not shown.
