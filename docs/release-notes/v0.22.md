## 0.22.0 — 2026-08-28

### Feature: Public staff & leadership directory

**Value:** A visitor to a congregation's or presbytery's public website had no way to see who's on staff or who leads the organization — that information either lived only behind the member sign-in, or had to be manually retyped onto the public site and kept in sync by hand. Staff and officer records are now the live source of truth for an optional public "who serves here" page.

**What's New:**
- From the Staff and Officers admin tools, an admin can mark a specific staff position or officer term "public" — a deliberate, per-person opt-in, off by default.
- A confirmation dialog explains plainly what turning this on means: the person's name, role, and photo (once photo support ships) become visible to anyone on the internet, including search engines, and turning it back off stops future display but doesn't retract anything already cached elsewhere.
- A public visitor sees only name, role/title, and department — never phone, email, or any other contact information, enforced independently at two layers so one mistake can't leak contact details.
- The listing updates automatically when a position or term ends — no separate step to remove someone from the public page.

**Permissions**

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `staff.manage` | Marking a staff position public or private |
| `officers.manage` | Marking an officer term public or private |

**Scope:** Ships behind `sites.public_staff_directory`, seeded off. Photo display is supported by the underlying design but not yet wired up — every listing shows name, role, and department only until a future release adds photo upload.

### Enhancement: Directory avatars, sub-tabs, and navigation get a visual refresh

**Value:** The Directory's people grid, its Members/Households/Parishes sub-tabs, and the top navigation all looked flatter and more uniform than they needed to — every person's avatar was an identical circle, the sub-tabs were plain buttons, and the top navigation had no icons.

**Changes:**
- Each person's avatar now gets its own color, drawn consistently from the same small palette every time that person appears anywhere in the app.
- The Members/Households/Parishes sub-tabs now sit inside a rounded tray with the active tab raised, replacing the previous plain-button styling.
- The top navigation now shows a small icon next to each section, and the active section is highlighted with a filled pill instead of an underline.
