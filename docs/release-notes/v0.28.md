## 0.28.0 — 2026-09-26

### A congregation can withdraw a statistical report it published to its presbytery

**Background:** Once a congregation published its annual statistical report, there was no way to take it back — a mistaken filing stayed the presbytery's figure of record until a corrected one was published over it. The database has carried a withdrawal shape since the publication tables shipped; nothing could write it.

**Changes:**
- A congregation's stated clerk can now see the history of the annual statistical reports it has filed with its presbytery and, citing the session minute that authorized it, withdraw one filed in error. The original filing is never deleted; it stays on record, clearly marked as withdrawn with the date and the minute reference.
- Presbyteries see a withdrawn report excluded from that congregation's current-year totals (falling back to any figures the presbytery entered or imported itself) and can review the complete filing history, withdrawals included, for every member congregation. A per-capita bill already issued off a since-withdrawn report is left untouched and is tracked as a follow-up.
- Only the congregation that published a report can withdraw it, through one audited database act; a presbytery cannot withdraw a report addressed to it.
- The page ships behind a flag that is off by default, and a new congregation "Stated Clerk" role template carries the publish-and-withdraw permission. A what's-new note is owed when the flag is first turned on.

## 0.28.1 — 2026-09-28

### A presbytery clerk who picks a year before a congregation joined the presbytery now sees a plain explanation, not a database error

**Background:** On the presbytery's reports page, a clerk enters each congregation's annual statistics for a chosen year. If the year was before that congregation joined the presbytery, the database correctly refused the entry, but the page showed the raw database message and the Save button stayed stuck on "Saving…". The anonymous filing link's generic error also implied a form-field problem when the real reason was the year.

**Changes:**
- The year field now knows each congregation's affiliation window and says so beneath the field; an out-of-window year is explained before anything is sent.
- If the database still refuses (for example a clerk working around the field), the page shows a plain sentence naming the congregation and the year, and the Save button resets.
- The anonymous filing link's generic error wording now covers "this year cannot be filed to this presbytery" without revealing anything more to an unauthenticated visitor.
- No change to what is saved or who may save it.

## 0.28.2 — 2026-09-28

### The automated browser tests now cover the presbytery portal, and the CI job that runs them can actually run

**Background:** The browser test suite touched three portal pages and none of the presbytery's own tools, so it could prove nothing about the portal a presbytery will use. Four existing tests had also gone stale, and the automated job meant to run the suite in CI had a configuration gap that would have made it fail on its first real run.

**Changes:**
- New browser tests walk a presbytery clerk through congregation oversight, statistics and per-capita entry, and ministry credentials, and walk a congregation clerk through publishing and withdrawing a statistical report while the presbytery sees each state. Every surface is also checked for its three refusal states: feature off, wrong kind of organization, and no permission.
- The tests create and clean up their own data and never touch the shared development fixtures, so running them cannot damage anything else.
- Three stale tests were repaired; one flaky test could not be reproduced and stays on the watch list.
- The CI job's missing setting is fixed. The job still waits on two credentials the operator adds before it runs.
