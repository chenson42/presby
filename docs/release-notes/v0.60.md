## 0.60.1 — 2026-11-17

### Security: Next.js 16.4.3 → 16.4.6

**Background:** Two moderate Next.js advisories (GHSA-mfr8-8qww-v76h, GHSA-vc7v-qv4w-wrv8) were published against 16.4.3 and fixed in 16.4.5. Both concern a caching feature PresbyPortal does not use, so nothing was exposed. The same release line's start-up upgrade notice had also begun stopping local development servers on many starts, which blocked local browser testing.

**Changes:**
- `next` and `eslint-config-next` move to 16.4.6. Nothing else in the lockfile moves.
- Nothing a member, administrator or visitor sees changes.

**Help:** no impact. No page, label, permission or flow changes.

## 0.60.0 — 2026-11-17

### Feature: Congregation map on the presbytery dashboard

**Value:** A presbytery's staff can see at a glance where its congregations are and how each is doing, on the same page as the dashboard cards.

#### What's New

- A "Congregation map" section on a presbytery's "Insights & Analytics" page, above the viability list. Each congregation with a location is a pin whose shape shows its rating: a diamond for "At risk", a square for "Fair", a circle for "Healthy", a hollow circle for "Not yet assessed". A key under the map says this in words.
- Choosing a pin shows the congregation's name and rating, with an "Open oversight page" link where Congregation Oversight is turned on.
- A line under the map says how many congregations have a location, and "Not on the map" lists the ones without one. When none has a location yet, the section explains how to add one instead of showing an empty map.
- On a phone, one finger scrolls the page past the map; two fingers or the + and - buttons move and zoom it.
- Only staff who can manage congregation oversight see the map; everyone else sees no map and receives no locations.
- The oversight page's "Map location" fields now explain what to type (decimal degrees, latitude first, a negative longitude in the United States) and accept a pasted pair such as "39.9612, -82.9988". Saving refuses a half-filled pair, a value that is not a number, and 0, 0.
- The map pictures come from MapTiler, credited to MapTiler and OpenStreetMap contributors. The viewer's browser asks MapTiler for them directly; no names, ratings or records are sent. The privacy policy now says so.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `congregation_oversight.manage` (existing) | Seeing the map and entering a location |

No new permission, flag, route or migration. The map appears only where the operator has set a MapTiler key (`NEXT_PUBLIC_MAPTILER_KEY`); preview deployments show the list without map pictures.

**Help:** /help/presbytery-reports (the map, and putting a congregation on it), /help/privacy-policy (MapTiler)
