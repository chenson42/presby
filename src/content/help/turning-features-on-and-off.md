Features are the optional parts of PresbyPortal. An administrator chooses which ones a congregation uses, and a new congregation starts with most of them turned off.

## Opening the Features page

1. Open your congregation's page. On a phone, open the menu with the button that has three lines.
2. Choose "Administration". The page is headed "Organization Administration".
3. Choose "Features".

If you see "You don't have permission to manage features", your role does not include it. Ask your stated clerk or another administrator. See [Why can't I see this?](/help/why-cant-i-see-this).

## What the page shows

The page is headed "Features". It has two parts.

- "Ministry areas": one switch for each of Community, Worship & Events, Giving & Finance, Governance & Courts, Reports & Insights and Communications. All of them are on at first. Turn one off if it does not apply to your congregation.
- Below it, a card for each feature, with its name, a sentence about what it does and a switch.

When a ministry area is off, the features in it say, for example, "Turn on Worship & Events above to enable this." and their switches are disabled.

The congregation in this picture has already turned on its features. A new congregation sees most of the cards switched off.

After a change, the card shows "Last changed" with the date and the address of the person who changed it. A short message such as "Tiered sensitive information turned on." confirms each change.

## Which features start on

This table lists the features a new congregation sees. Your Features page may list more. Each card says what its feature does.

| Feature | What it does | At first | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community: add people and record roll actions | Lets your administrators add people, record participants and propose roll actions. | Off | Any organization |
| Connect people when they sign in | Connects a person's sign-in to their record when they sign in with the email address on it, so they don't need an invitation. | On | Any organization |
| Tiered sensitive information | Lets your congregation enter pastoral notes, demographics, medical and allergy information, and disability records, subject to each viewer's own permissions. | Off | Any organization |
| Member calendar | Shows the congregation's events to every member who has calendar access. | On | Any organization |
| Public events calendar | Shows the events your team has marked for the website on your public website. Whoever maintains your website must also place the calendar on a page. | Off | Any organization |
| Job openings | Lets your congregation post job openings on its public website. Whoever maintains your website can also place the openings on a page. | Off | Any organization |
| Giving | Online giving for the congregation. The card on the Features page describes it, and this help does not cover giving. | Off | Congregations and new worshiping communities |
| Parish management | Deacon districts for the congregation. | Off | Congregations |

## What you see when a feature is on or off

Turning a feature on or off changes what you see in two ways.

- Some features show their tile only while they are on. The "Calendar" and "Job openings" tiles are examples. Open "Administration" or your congregation's page and the tile appears or disappears.
- Others keep their tile and open a page that says the feature is off. For example, "Community" says "Member management isn't turned on" for your congregation until you turn on "Community: add people and record roll actions".

## Features the PresbyPortal team turns on for you

"Website domain" lets a congregation connect its own web address to its public website. It is not on the Features page. If it is off, the "Website domain" page says to ask PresbyPortal support to turn it on. {{contact}}

## What turning a feature off does

Turning a feature off hides its pages and its tile. Turning it on again brings them back. Some cards say more. The "Parish management" card says that nothing already assigned is removed when you turn it off.
