After you sign in, PresbyPortal decides where to send you. This page explains the three places you can land and how to move between congregations.

## After you sign in

- If you belong to one congregation, you go straight to that congregation's page.
- If you belong to more than one organization, you land on your home page, which lists them.
- If you belong to none, you see a page that explains why. See [Why can't I see this?](/help/why-cant-i-see-this#not-connected-to-a-congregation).

If you opened a link to a page inside a congregation before you signed in, you return to that page after you sign in.

## Your home page

Your home page says "Welcome" and your name. Under it you may find:

- "Your organizations": a card for each organization you can open, with its name and its kind, such as "Congregation". Select a card to open that organization. Your home page never opens one for you.
- "Still being set up": organizations you are connected to that are not ready yet.
- "Quick links": the "Account settings" button. See [Your account](/help/your-account).
- "What's new": the latest news about PresbyPortal, with a "See all" link.
- A card that asks "How are we doing?". See [Getting help](/help/getting-help).

From "Account settings", the "Home" link at the top left opens your home page.

## Switching congregations

Inside a congregation, the congregation's name is at the top of the page.

- If you belong to more than one organization, a button with two small arrows sits beside the name. Select it to open a menu headed "Switch organization". Choose another organization from the list to open it, or choose "Go to your home page".
- If you belong to only one organization, the name is plain text and there is nothing to switch to.

If you type the address of a congregation you do not belong to, you see a message that you do not have access. See [Why can't I see this?](/help/why-cant-i-see-this#a-congregation-says-you-do-not-have-access).

## Signing out

Select your initials at the top right, then choose "Sign out".

- Signing out inside a congregation takes you to that congregation's public website. If it does not have one, you see "Page not found".
- Signing out from your home page or from "Account settings" takes you to the PresbyPortal home page.
