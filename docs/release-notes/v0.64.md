## 0.64.0 — 2026-11-19

### Feature: Administrative commission records

**Value:** A presbytery can keep its own record of each administrative commission it appoints: what it was charged with, which congregation it concerns, when and by what minute it was appointed, who serves on it, and when it reported. That is the tracking the presbytery needs at go-live, kept beside its committees.

#### What's New

- **An "Administrative commissions" section on Committees & Commissions.** Use "Record a commission" to enter:
  - a title;
  - a charge, in general terms, up to 500 characters. The form says not to record confidential details.
  - optionally one of the presbytery's member congregations, or none;
  - the date it was appointed, which can't be after today;
  - a minute reference.
- **Members** use the same titles as committees: Chair, Vice chair, Clerk, Secretary, Ex officio and Member. Each has a start date and an optional end date. A member's term can be ended on its own.
- **End commission.** Records the end date and the minute where the commission reported. Every member's term ends that day. Ended commissions leave the default list, and "Show ended commissions" brings them back.
- **Correcting and removing.** "Edit commission" corrects a record, including one that has ended. "Remove this record" takes a mistaken record out of view. It can't be undone from the page.
- **A record gives no one access.** It gives no one access to a congregation's PresbyPortal, and the congregation it concerns does not see it. Every page says so.
- **Privacy.**
  - Anyone at the presbytery who can manage committees can read a commission's charge.
  - Reading a record is not recorded in the audit trail.
  - Changes are recorded, but the audit trail never copies the title, charge or minute text.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `groups.manage` (existing; in the presbytery founding-administrator bundle) | Everything on Committees & Commissions, including commission records |
