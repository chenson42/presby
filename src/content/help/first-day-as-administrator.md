This page is for the Founding Administrator: the person the PresbyPortal team invited to set up your congregation. It shows where you land, what you can do yourself on the first day, what needs the PresbyPortal team, and a sensible order for setting up.

## Where you land

After you sign in, you go straight to your congregation's page when it is your only congregation. If you belong to more than one, see [Your home page and switching congregations](/help/home-and-switching-congregations).

The page opens with a greeting, "Find a person" and "Coming up". Below them are tiles under headings such as "Community", "Worship & Events" and "Governance & Courts". On a phone, the menu is the button with three lines.

The picture shows the top of the page on a phone, with the tiles under "Community" and the start of "Worship & Events".

![The top of a congregation's page on a phone: tiles under Community, then Worship and Events.](shot:portal-tiles)

Every tile is shown whatever your role. A tile you cannot use opens a page that says you don't have permission. [Why can't I see this?](/help/why-cant-i-see-this) explains each case. Three tiles open a page that only describes an idea, and nothing on them works: "Worship & Service Planning", "Insights & Analytics" and "Communications".

## The Administration area

1. Open the menu and choose "Administration".
2. The page is headed "Organization Administration". In a new congregation it lists "Manage events", "Roles", "Tickets", "Features" and "Branding".

The congregation in this picture has already turned on several features, so its page also lists "Manage giving" and "Website domain".

## What you can do yourself

This is what a Founding Administrator can use on the first day. A Founding Administrator also holds the Stated Clerk role.

You can use these:

- "Features": turn optional features on and off. See [Turning features on and off](/help/turning-features-on-and-off).
- "Community": add people and record how each is connected. Turn on "Community: add people and record roll actions" first. See [Adding people and recording members](/help/adding-people).
- "Officers": record officers and the Session. See [Officers and the Session](/help/officers-and-the-session).
- "Roles": give roles and take them away. See [Roles: who can do what](/help/roles-who-can-do-what).
- "Groups": committees, small groups, choirs and teams.
- "Staff": paid, non-ordained staff positions.
- "Directory": search for people. See [Using the directory](/help/using-the-directory).
- "Calendar": read the congregation's events. It is on from the start.
- "Job openings": write and publish job openings for your website. Turn on "Job openings" first. See [Your congregation's website](/help/your-congregations-website).
- "Tickets": ask the PresbyPortal team for help. See [Getting help](/help/getting-help).

You cannot use these yet:

- "Manage events": add events to the calendar. The page says "You don't have permission to manage events". See [Events and the calendar](/help/events-and-the-calendar).
- "Branding": your colour, type pairing and logo. The page says "You don't have permission to manage branding".
- "Website domain": your own web address for your website. It is not on the Features page. The PresbyPortal team turns it on.

For these three, the PresbyPortal team sets things up. {{contactAdmin}}

## Turn on what you need

A new congregation starts with the calendar on and most other features off. Open "Features" and turn on what you use. The page lists each feature and what it does. A switch takes effect straight away, and a message such as "Job openings turned on." confirms it.

## A sensible order

1. Open "Features" and turn on "Community: add people and record roll actions". Turn on "Job openings" too if you want to post them.
2. Open "Officers" and record the members of your Session. At least one of them needs a connected sign-in before anyone can approve a new member. You can record yourself. To connect the others, invite them. See [Inviting people to sign in](/help/sign-in-invitations).
3. Open "Community" and add your people. Invite the ones who should sign in. See [Inviting people to sign in](/help/sign-in-invitations).
4. Open "Roles" and give the roles that help you share the work.
5. If you need to add events or set your colours, ask for that role. {{contactAdmin}}
