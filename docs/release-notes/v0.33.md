## 0.33.1 — 2026-10-19

### Security: Updated the web framework and two supporting libraries

**Background:** Security advisories were published against the web framework the platform is built on (Next.js) and two libraries it relies on (one for resizing images, one for processing stylesheets). The framework advisories covered image handling, page caching and a few developer-only tools. The fixes are small patch releases, so nothing about how the platform looks or behaves changes.

**Changes:**
- Next.js 16.3.6 to 16.3.8, with its matching lint tooling.
- The image-processing library (sharp) 0.35.4 to 0.35.5 and the stylesheet library (source-map-js) 1.2.1 to 1.2.2.
- Two low-severity advisories remain in the documentation diagram tooling (mermaid and katex). They affect only developer documentation, not the congregation-facing platform, and fixing them needs a larger upgrade that is tracked separately.

## 0.33.0 — 2026-10-15

### Feature: The portal home shows your groups and what is coming up

**Value:** The portal home page showed a greeting, a search box and the tools your congregation has switched on. It did not show you what was coming up at church, and its household card could say "2 members" for a household where one person had since died.

#### What's New

The home page now has two cards side by side on a larger screen (stacked on a phone). **Yours** shows your household, counted the same way the directory counts it, and only when at least two people are in it, plus the groups you belong to. **Coming up** shows the next four events, one for each repeating event, and links to the full calendar; it appears only when your congregation has turned the calendar on and you are allowed to see it. If only one card has something to show, it takes the full width. The search box now appears only for people who are allowed to use the directory. If the events or groups cannot be loaded for a moment, only that card is affected and the rest of the page still loads.

- Nothing about the tool tiles changes: they are still shown by what your congregation has switched on, never hidden by what you personally may open, so a tile you can see can still tell you that you don't have access when you open it.

#### Permissions

| Permission | Required for |
|------------|--------------|
| `directory.view` (existing, baseline) | the search box and the household card |
| `events.view` or `events.manage` (existing, from 0.32.0) | the Coming up card |
| none | your own groups — a member always sees the groups they belong to |

#### New Routes

None. `/o/<slug>` is the existing portal home.
