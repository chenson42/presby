## 0.27.0 — 2026-09-26

### The database can now remember a congregation's past names, and hold an unmatched spreadsheet row for review instead of dropping it

**Background:** A presbytery's historical records use whatever name a congregation was known by at the time — churches merge, rename, and are recorded under handwritten variants in decades-old paper reports. Matching those old records to today's congregations by name alone is unreliable, and past attempts to import that kind of data simply threw away anything that did not match cleanly. This release lays the groundwork to do both properly, and changes nothing anyone will see yet.

**Changes:**
- The database can now store more than one name for a congregation over time — its current name, any former official names, and looser historical or handwritten variants — each dated and optionally tied to a city, so a name from 1965 can be told apart from a same-named church founded later elsewhere. Every existing organization received its current name as a starting entry.
- A new lookup searches across that whole history to suggest which congregation an old record probably refers to, ranked by a plain confidence band, and never exposes a congregation's platform account status.
- The database gained a place to hold a spreadsheet row that cannot be matched automatically: the original data, what was tried, and why it is unresolved, kept permanently rather than silently skipped.
- Nothing is reachable from any screen yet — no upload page, no review screen, and no way to record a name change. This release is the storage and the matching logic only; the pages that use them ship later.
