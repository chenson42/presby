## 0.59.0 — 2026-11-17

### Feature: Presbytery dashboard

**Value:** A presbytery's staff can see how its congregations are doing on one page, without opening four reports.

#### What's New

- A presbytery's "Insights & Analytics" tile, on the portal home under "Reports & Insights", now opens a dashboard instead of a "coming soon" page. Congregations, synods and the General Assembly still see "coming soon".
- Four cards: member congregations, ending active members (the newest year with statistics, with how many congregations reported), vacant pulpits, and per capita billed, paid and still owed for this year.
- A viability list, at-risk congregations first, each linking to its oversight page.
- Each card shows only to staff who hold the matching report's permission; the others show "Locked". Someone who holds none of the four sees one message saying which permissions open the page.
- Where nothing has been recorded the card says so, never a bare zero. A card that fails to load says so and offers "Try again" without blanking the rest.
- Links and "record it here" instructions appear only where the page they point to is switched on.
- The map of congregations is not in this release; it follows once a map key is set up.

#### Permissions

No new permission. Each card uses its report's existing key: `statistics.manage`, `credentials.manage`, `per_capita.manage`, `congregation_oversight.manage`.

#### New Routes

None. `/o/<slug>/admin/insights` already existed as a "coming soon" page and is gated by the existing `org_portal.insights` flag.

**Help:** /help/presbytery-reports (new "Insights and analytics" section).
