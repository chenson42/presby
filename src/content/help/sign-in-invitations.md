A person you record in Community has a record, but a record is not a sign-in. A person sees your congregation's pages only when their sign-in is connected to their record. A sign-in gets connected in one of two ways. Either the person accepts an invitation you send, or the person signs in with the email address on their record and PresbyPortal connects it. This page covers both.

Someone who signs in without a connection sees a page headed "You're not connected to a congregation yet". See [Why can't I see this?](/help/why-cant-i-see-this).

## Send an invitation

1. Open "Community" and choose a person, then "Edit person". See [Adding people and recording members](/help/adding-people).
2. Find the card headed "Sign in to PresbyPortal". It invites you to invite the person to sign in, names the email address on file, and says that the link works once.
3. Select "Send invitation". A dialog asks "Send an invitation to" the person's name, with a question mark. It repeats the address and the date the link stops working, and says to send it only if the address belongs to that person, because whoever opens the link becomes that person on PresbyPortal. Confirm with "Send invitation".

The link works once and stops working after seven days. The email names your congregation and, usually, you as the sender. You cannot add a message to it.

A person with no email address on file has no button: the card says to add one under "Contact & address", then come back.

Two people need an extra step:

- If the person is recorded as a visitor, the dialog tells you that anyone who signs in can see your member directory and your calendar, with each person's privacy settings applied. Tick "I understand, send it anyway" to continue.
- If the person asked not to be contacted at the address, the dialog says this is a one-time account email, not marketing, and asks you to tick the same box.

### After you send it

The card now says "Invitation sent to" the address "on" a date, and "It expires" a date. A word beside it, "Queued", "Sent" or "Delivered", says how far the email has got. While the invitation is open it says that signing in does not connect the person by itself. They accept the invitation instead.

- "Send again" sends a new link and turns off the earlier one. You can send again five minutes after the last send, and the card shows how long is left. After several sends PresbyPortal stops sending more, and suggests asking the person to check their spam folder or sending a ticket.
- "Cancel invitation" turns the link off. Confirm in the dialog "Cancel this invitation?", or choose "Keep it".
- If the email bounced, the card says "The email to" the address "bounced. Check the address on file, fix it, and send again." If the link expired, the card offers "Send a new one".
- If the address on the record changed after you sent, the earlier link no longer works. Send again to reach the new address.

Some invitations are refused, and the page says why. A few examples: the person can already sign in (they can use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page); the address is yours, or is shared with another person at your congregation, because each person needs their own address; the person's connection to your congregation has ended; the person holds a seat on the Session or the Board of Deacons and you cannot manage officers; or the person holds access you do not hold yourself, so you ask an administrator who does. If you send too many in a day, the page says "Too many invitations right now. Try again later."

### In the Community list

A person's row can carry a small marker that links to their card: "Not connected yet", "Invitation sent" and a date, "Invitation expired. Send again", "Email bounced. Check the address", or "Invitation couldn't be completed". A person with no address is marked "Not connected yet. No email on file". Only people who can manage people see the markers.

## What the person sees

The email is headed "You're invited to PresbyPortal". See [Accepting your invitation](/help/accepting-your-invitation) for the whole path.

- A person with no account opens the link, sees a page headed "You're invited" that says your congregation invited them to sign in, and creates an account with their name and a password. The name boxes are empty; the page does not name the person before they sign in.
- A person with an account signs in with that address and accepts. If they sign in with that address instead of opening the email, the invitation is waiting for them on a page headed "You're invited to" and your congregation's name, with a button "Accept the invitation".

Accepting connects the sign-in to the record. It adds no role. Any role or seat the person already holds on your congregation's records then takes effect. After they accept, the card says "Connected through an invitation on" a date. To change who signs in as that person, send a support ticket. See [Getting help](/help/getting-help).

## Signing in with the address on file

Many people connect without an invitation. "Connect people when they sign in" is a card on the Features page, and it is on by default. When a person signs in with the exact email address on their record, PresbyPortal connects their sign-in to that record if it can. The card says "When" the person "signs in with" the address, "PresbyPortal connects them to this record if it can. An invitation also connects them." After it happens, the card says "Connected through sign-in on" a date.

It does not connect everyone. Among the people it does not connect are:

- a person recorded as a visitor. The card says that signing in won't connect them by itself;
- a person whose email address is also on another person's record. Each person needs their own address;
- a person who has more than one current connection to a congregation;
- a person whose address is on an open invitation. The invitation comes first;
- anyone, if you turn "Connect people when they sign in" off. Then invitations still work and people already connected stay connected.

The card says so under its heading for a visitor, a shared address, and when the option is off. This option adds no role. The person gets whatever access their record already carries.

### People who hold a role or a seat

A person who holds a role, or a seat on the Session or the Board of Deacons, is connected only after they confirm two-factor sign-in. They sign in, see "You're not connected to a congregation yet", and select "Check again". If they have not yet confirmed two-factor sign-in, the page says "One more step: confirm two-factor sign-in to finish connecting. Then come back here and select Check again." Its button is "Continue to verification", or "Set up two-factor sign-in" if they have not set it up. When they come back and select "Check again", they are connected. See [Setting up two-factor sign-in](/help/setting-up-two-factor).

## Session members and approval

A new member waits for the Session to approve. A member of the Session approves only after their sign-in is connected. See [Officers and the Session](/help/officers-and-the-session).

When no Session member can approve, "Pending approvals" says why. It says, for example, that members of the Session "are recorded, but none has connected a sign-in yet, so no one can approve". A person who can manage officers and people also sees a row for each Session member: "Not invited yet" with an "Invite" link, "Invitation sent" and a date, "No email on file", "Email bounced", "Invitation expired" or "Invitation couldn't be completed". The page counts the invitations sent to your Session members. Someone who can manage people but not officers sees "Ask an administrator to invite your Session members to sign in."

If members of the Session have connected a sign-in but none holds permission to approve roll actions, the page says so and suggests asking an administrator to check the Session member role. See [Roles: who can do what](/help/roles-who-can-do-what).
