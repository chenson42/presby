Account settings is where you change how you sign in. Everyone who signs in has an account, whether or not they belong to a congregation.

## Opening your account settings

Select your initials at the top right of any page, then choose "Account". On your home page there is also an "Account settings" button under "Quick links".

This page covers four sections of "Account settings": "Profile", "Email address", "Password" and "Two-factor authentication".

## Changing your name

1. In "Profile", change "Display name".
2. Select "Save name".

The page says "Name updated." Your display name is the name shown in the account menu and in the welcome on your home page.

## Changing your email address

Your email address is the address you sign in with. To change it:

1. In "Email address", type the new address under "New email address".
2. Select "Request email change".
3. The page says "Check your new inbox for a verification link." The section then shows "Pending change" with the new address and a "Cancel" button. Select "Cancel" if you change your mind.
4. Open the message sent to the new address and use the link in it. The link works for 24 hours.

Your sign-in email changes only when you use the link. Until then, "Current email" still shows the old address and you keep signing in with it.

After you use the link, the page you land on may say "Something went wrong" even though the change was made. Open "Account settings" and look at "Current email" to see the new address.

You see "That email is already in use." if another account already uses the new address.

## Changing your password

The "Password" section appears only on accounts that have a password. An account made through Google does not have one.

1. In "Password", type your "Current password".
2. Type your "New password" and type it again under "Confirm new password". A password needs at least 8 characters.
3. Select "Change password".

The page says "Password updated." If you see "Current password is incorrect." or "Passwords do not match.", check what you typed. If you do not know your current password, use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page. See [Signing in](/help/signing-in).

## Two-factor authentication

The "Two-factor authentication" section shows "Active" when an authenticator app is linked to your account and "Not set up" when it is not. The link beside it, "Manage / regenerate codes" or "Set up two-factor authentication", opens the page for it. See [Setting up two-factor sign-in](/help/setting-up-two-factor).
