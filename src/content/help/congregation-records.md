Where "Congregation Oversight" is turned on, a presbytery keeps its own record of each member congregation. This page is for the presbytery's staff. The record is the presbytery's own copy: the congregation does not see it, and nothing here changes what the congregation has recorded for itself.

Open your presbytery's page, choose "Congregation Oversight" under "Governance & Courts", then choose "Open" beside a congregation. The page has five sections: "Profile", "Assessment", "Buildings", "Insurance" and "Loans".

## Who can see what

Two permissions decide it, and having one does not give you the other.

| Section | What you can do | Permission you need |
|---|---|---|
| "Profile", "Assessment" and "Buildings" | See and change them | Manage congregation oversight |
| "Insurance", "Loans", and the "Appraisals" on each building | See and change them | Manage congregation property finances |

A building's page belongs to "Buildings", so a person needs the first permission to open it and the second to see its "Appraisals". A section you cannot open says "Locked" and names the permission. On the list, a column you cannot see is left out, and a note above the table says which permission it needs. A presbytery's Founding Administrator has both permissions from the first day. To give the second one to someone else, such as your treasurer or stated clerk, create a role under "Roles" that includes the permission described as "See and record a member congregation's appraisals, insurance and loans (the presbytery's own record)", and give it to them. See [Roles: who can do what](/help/roles-who-can-do-what). Anyone who holds it is asked to set up two-factor sign-in the next time they sign in, if they have not already. See [Setting up two-factor sign-in](/help/setting-up-two-factor).

## Profile

The profile holds the address, city, county, year organized and phone. Choose "Add profile details" (or "Edit profile"), fill in what you know, and choose "Save". Nothing is required. Type the address as you would write it on an envelope. A congregation that is on PresbyPortal can keep its own profile there; the profile here is the presbytery's own copy, so changing it does not change theirs, and theirs does not change it.

## Assessment

The assessment is the presbytery's rating of the congregation (at risk, fair or healthy), its redevelopment notes, its property notes and its map location. Choose "Edit assessment". For the map location, see [Presbytery reports and downloads](/help/presbytery-reports#putting-a-congregation-on-the-map).

## Buildings

Choose "Add a building". A building has a name and a type, and everything else is optional: the year built and up to two addition years, the square feet, the condition of the roof, plumbing, heating and cooling and boiler, whether it has sprinklers, a fire alarm and a security system, and notes. Only one building can be the "Primary building". Choose "Open" on a building to see the same facts on its own page. Choose "Retire this building" for one that was sold, demolished or closed: it leaves the active list, its history stays, and "Bring back into use" undoes it. "Remove (entered by mistake)" is for a building that should never have been recorded. A removed building, and its appraisals, are no longer listed, and the page offers no way to undo it.

## Appraisals, insurance and loans

These need the finance permission. On a building's page, under "Appraisals", choose "Record an appraisal" with the year assessed and the amount in dollars; the newest year is marked "Current". To correct an appraisal, remove it and record it again. Under "Insurance", choose "Record a year" for the policy year, carrier, policy number, expiry date, recent claims and interest in the insurance pool. There is one record for each year. A policy whose expiry date has passed says "Expired" in words. Under "Loans", choose "Record a loan": the kind (a presbytery program loan, a guaranteed outside loan or another kind), the lender, the original amount, the current balance and the date it is as of, any grants, and a note about the guarantee. "Mark paid off" sets the balance to $0 as of the date you choose, and "Reopen this loan" undoes it. A section with nothing in it says so and never shows a zero for something that was not recorded.

Changes are recorded in the audit trail with who made them, and the notes and amounts are not copied into it. A change to the "Assessment" is recorded just after it is saved, so in rare cases a saved assessment can be missing from the trail. Looking at a record is not recorded.

## If something looks wrong

- A page that says Congregation Oversight "isn't turned on": the PresbyPortal team switches it on for the whole platform. [Contact the team](/help/contact) if you need it.
- A section that says "Locked": you do not have the permission it names. Ask an administrator to give it under "Roles".
- "This record was changed by someone else after you opened it.": another person saved first. Reload the page, then make your change again.
- A section that fails to load has a "Try again" link, and the rest of the page still works.
- A congregation that has left your presbytery is no longer listed. Its records are kept, but this page cannot open them. A congregation that has closed but is still a member is still listed.
- A record marked "Imported from an earlier record" was loaded from your presbytery's earlier spreadsheet. It stays marked after you edit it.
