## 0.26.0 — 2026-09-25

### A presbytery can email a congregation with no account a one-time link to file its annual statistical report

**Background:** Many congregations in a presbytery have no account on the platform and never will, but the presbytery still needs their annual statistical report. Until now the presbytery's clerk had to enter those figures by hand. This release lets the clerk send the congregation a link instead.

**Changes:**
- On the presbytery's reports page, a clerk with the statistics permission can issue a submission link for a member congregation and a report year, addressed to a named person by email, and can see whether each link is outstanding, expired, revoked or filed. Links expire, can be revoked, and only one can be outstanding per congregation and year. Links can only be issued for congregations the presbytery actually holds, and only for congregations that do not manage their own records on the platform — those file through their own portal.
- The recipient opens the link, sees the report form for that year, fills it in, states their name and office, and submits once. Nothing is saved if the submission fails, and a link that has expired, been used, or been revoked shows the same plain notice with no details.
- A filed report appears to the presbytery exactly as a report the congregation published itself would: as a published record it can read, with the figures the recipient attested.
- The feature ships switched off and is turned on per platform when a presbytery is ready.
- Behind the scenes: the report, its publication and the presbytery's copy are now written by one shared database routine whether a congregation publishes itself or files by link, so the two paths cannot drift apart. Issuing links and filing are both rate-limited.
