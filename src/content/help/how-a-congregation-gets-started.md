There is no sign-up form. A congregation does not make its own account on PresbyPortal. The PresbyPortal team sets the congregation up and invites its first administrator by email.

## How a congregation is set up

1. A PresbyPortal administrator adds the congregation.
2. The same administrator invites the congregation's first administrator by email. That person is called the founding administrator.
3. The founding administrator opens the link in the email. They create an account, or sign in if they already have an account that uses the same email address.
4. The founding administrator turns on the features the congregation wants and adds its people. See [Turning features on and off](/help/turning-features-on-and-off).
5. The administrator can invite the people they record to sign in. See [Inviting people to sign in](/help/sign-in-invitations).

## What the invitation is

The invitation is an email with a link. The link works once, and it works only for the email address it was sent to.

The page the link opens is headed "You're invited". It shows only part of the email address. On that page you either create an account or, if you already have an account that uses that address, sign in and accept. A new account needs a first name, a last name and a password of at least 8 characters.

If the link no longer works, the page says "This link isn't active". It does not say why.

After you accept, you may be asked to set up two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app. Whether you are asked depends on your account, your role and your congregation.

## Asking about PresbyPortal for your congregation

PresbyPortal does not have a contact form. The way to reach the people who run it is the project's public page, [PresbyPortal on GitHub](https://github.com/chenson42/presby), where you can open an issue to ask a question. Opening an issue needs a GitHub account. What you write there is public, so leave out personal details and your congregation's private information.
