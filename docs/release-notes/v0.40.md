## 0.40.0 — 2026-10-23

### Feature: a footer that matches the menu, and "Community" everywhere

**Value:** The menu at the top of a portal page and the footer at the bottom used to name things differently, which made it hard to tell they led to the same places. They now come from one list, so they always use the same names, and the footer is laid out the way many websites lay out a site map: a column for each section, with its tools underneath.

#### What's New

- **The footer is a site map.** Each section of the menu is a column heading, with that section's tools listed beneath it. Home is a link of its own above the columns. On a phone the columns wrap to two across, and every link is large enough to tap comfortably. Your church's contact details and the copyright line are unchanged.
- **"People & Membership" is now called "Community."** The section keeps its place and its tools; only the name changed. It reads "Community" in the menu, on the portal home page, in the footer, and as a category on the Features page.
- **The menu and the footer can no longer disagree.** Both are built from the same list, so a section appears in both or in neither. A tool that is not built yet shows its name followed by "Coming soon."
- **The Administration link now follows one rule.** It appears in the menu and in the footer only when your congregation has at least one administration tool turned on. Before, the menu showed it whenever the administration area was turned on, even if it had nothing in it. In the footer, Administration is a heading with one link, "Administration overview," that opens the administration page.
- **Everyone sees the Administration link when it is shown.** Which tools a person can actually use is still decided on each page behind it, so someone without the right access will see that page's "not allowed" message instead of its tools.
- **On the portal home page, the "Community" menu item and the "Community" card are separate links.** The menu item scrolls to the Community section of the page; the card opens the Community page. That is expected.

The footer still appears only where your organization's portal has it turned on.

**Technical:** `buildPortalMenu()` in `src/lib/org-portal/portal-menu.ts` is the single structure, loaded by `getPortalMenu()` in `src/lib/org-portal/visible-tiles.ts`, and rendered by `portal-nav.tsx` and `src/components/org-portal/portal-footer.tsx`. The `people` domain key, its `#domain-people` anchor and the stored category value are unchanged; no schema change. DECISION-220–222. Work-log: `docs/work-log/2026-10-23-portal-sitemap-footer.md`.
