## 0.61.0 — 2026-11-18

### Feature: Congregation records for presbyteries

**Value:** A presbytery keeps its own record of each member congregation (its property, insurance and loans) in one place, instead of in spreadsheets and filing cabinets. The record is the presbytery's own: the congregation never sees it, and nothing entered here changes what the congregation keeps for itself.

#### What's New

- On a presbytery, "Congregation Oversight" (under "Governance & Courts" on the portal home) now lists member congregations, and "Open" leads to one page per congregation with five sections: Profile, Assessment, Buildings, Insurance and Loans.
- **Profile:** address, city, county, year organized, phone and notes.
- **Buildings:** each building with its type, year built, size, the condition of its roof, plumbing, heating and boiler, and its safety systems. One building can be marked primary. A building that is sold or no longer used is retired (and can be brought back into use); a building entered by mistake is removed.
- **Appraisals:** a dated appraised value per building; the newest is marked "Current".
- **Insurance by year:** one record per year, with the carrier, coverage and expiry; an expired year is labelled "Expired" in words.
- **Loans:** the kind of loan, lender, balance as of a date, and guarantees. A loan can be marked paid off and reopened.
- The congregations list gains a buildings column ("1 building", "None in use", "Not recorded") and an insurance-expiry column, each shown only to people with the matching permission.
- Every page and form works on a phone. Changes are written to the audit trail with who made them; notes and amounts are not copied into the trail.
- The viability assessment form moved to its own page under the congregation's record ("Edit assessment").

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `congregation_oversight.manage` (existing) | Profile, assessment and buildings. Its description no longer mentions insurance. |
| `congregation_oversight.finance` (new, financial tier, requires two-factor sign-in) | Appraisals, insurance and loans |

A presbytery's Founding Administrator receives both permissions; this is the first financial permission in that role. They can give the new permission to others through Roles, and anyone who receives it is asked to set up two-factor sign-in. A person with only one of the two permissions sees the other sections as "Locked".

#### New Routes

- `/o/<slug>/admin/oversight/<congregation>`: the congregation's record, with full-page forms beneath it for the profile, the assessment, buildings, appraisals, insurance years and loans.

**Known limits:**
- A congregation that leaves the presbytery drops out of reach of these pages; its records are kept.
- Recording an appraisal needs both permissions.
- A change to the Assessment is recorded in the audit trail just after it is saved, so in rare cases a saved assessment can be missing from the trail.

**Deploy day (2026-11-18):** no old insurance details were stored in the assessment form, so nothing needed carrying over. One presbytery founding administrator received the new permission. Nobody was newly asked to set up two-factor sign-in.

Congregation Oversight must be switched on by the PresbyPortal team before presbyteries can reach these pages.

**Help:** /help/congregation-records (new); /help/roles-who-can-do-what, /help/why-cant-i-see-this, /help/presbytery-reports (the map location steps now start from "Edit assessment"), and the presbytery-tools placeholder.
