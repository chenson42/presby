## 0.21.0 — 2026-08-27

### Enhancement: A real visual identity

**Value:** The platform looked like a placeholder — a text wordmark reading "presby," a generic blue accent color, and a bare-bones favicon. PresbyPortal now has a real, considered identity: a logo, a navy-and-blue palette with a gold accent, and a proper typeface.

**Changes:**
- A real logo (mark + wordmark) replaces the plain text "presby"/"PresbyPortal" in the site header and the signed-in navigation bar, in both light and dark mode.
- The platform's accent color changed from a generic blue to PresbyPortal's own navy and blue, with a gold accent color reserved for the logo itself.
- The browser tab icon and the app icon (for anyone who adds the site to their home screen) both changed to the new mark.
- Body and heading text now render in Inter, a typeface chosen for legibility at small sizes.
- Every color still meets the platform's own accessibility floors (AAA body text, AA controls) — re-verified against the new palette, not assumed.

**Scope:** This is the platform's own identity — the marketing home page and the signed-in chrome around every page. A congregation's own branding on its portal and public site (set at `/o/<slug>/admin/branding`) is unaffected and still overrides the platform's colors and fonts exactly as it did before.

<a name="0.21.0-feature-categories"></a>
### Infrastructure: Ministry-area categories for feature administration

**Background:** `/o/<slug>/admin/features` already let an organization turn individual optional features on or off, but the catalog was small and growing — with no way to say "our congregation doesn't do worship planning" without hunting through an ever-longer list of individual toggles.

**Changes:**
- A new "Ministry areas" section on the features page lets an organization turn a whole area (People & Membership, Worship & Events, Giving & Finance, Governance & Courts, Reports & Insights, Communications) on or off, narrowing which individual features are shown as available underneath.
- Every ministry area is on by default — this only narrows things down when an organization explicitly says an area doesn't apply to them, and never silently removes something an organization already had turned on.
- Ships behind `org_portal.feature_categories`, seeded off — invisible until a future release turns it on for real organizations.

<a name="0.21.0-staff"></a>
### Feature: Staff and personnel record-keeping

**Value:** A congregation's or presbytery's paid staff — a bookkeeper, a part-time choir director, a custodian — didn't fit anywhere in the system. They aren't ordained officers, and many aren't members at all, so there was no way to record who's employed, in what role, and since when.

**What's New:**
- A new Staff tool records a paid position: title, department, and start/end dates, for anyone who works for the organization — whether or not they're a member.
- Adding someone new offers two paths: pick an existing person from the directory, or add someone who isn't in the system yet, without treating them as a new member.
- Ending a position is a soft close (an end date and reason), never a deletion — the record stays.
- This is record-keeping only for now: no salary or compensation information, and no public-facing staff directory. Both are natural next steps.

**Scope:** Ships behind `org_portal.staff`, seeded off — invisible until a future release turns it on for real organizations.
