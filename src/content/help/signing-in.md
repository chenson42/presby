To sign in, open PresbyPortal and choose "Sign in". You can sign in with Google or with the email address and password of your PresbyPortal account.

The sign-in page shows "Sign in with Google" first, then the word "or", then the email form.

## Signing in with email and password

1. Type your email address.
2. Type your password.
3. Select "Sign in with email".

If the email address and password match, you are signed in. You may then be asked for a code from your authenticator app. See [Using your code to sign in](/help/using-your-code). After that, PresbyPortal takes you to where you were going, or to your congregation or your home page. See [Your home page and switching congregations](/help/home-and-switching-congregations).

## Signing in with Google

1. Select "Sign in with Google".
2. Follow Google's steps.
3. You come back to PresbyPortal signed in.

If your PresbyPortal account uses the same email address as your Google account, Google signs you in to that same account.

If Google has not confirmed your email address, or your account has been deactivated, PresbyPortal refuses the sign-in and shows a page headed "Access Denied". If you have a password, use "Sign in with email" instead.

## If the sign-in does not work

The sign-in page says "Wrong email or password." when any of these is true:

- The email address or the password is wrong.
- There is no account for that email address.
- The account is locked.
- The account has been deactivated.

It never says which one. Check how you typed both, then read the sections below.

## If you forgot your password

1. Under the sign-in form, select "Forgot password?".
2. Type your email address and select "Send reset link".
3. The page says "Check your email". It says the same thing whether or not an account exists for that address.
4. Open the email and use the link in it. The link works for 60 minutes and works once.
5. On the page headed "Set new password", type a new password of at least 8 characters twice and select "Set new password".
6. The sign-in page opens with the message "Password updated. Sign in with your new password."

An account made through Google has no password, so no reset email is sent for it.

## If your account is locked

After five wrong passwords in a row, the account is locked for 15 minutes. While it is locked, even the right password shows "Wrong email or password."

Wait 15 minutes and try again, or reset your password as described above. Resetting your password also unlocks the account.

## If your account has been deactivated

A deactivated account cannot sign in. The sign-in page shows "Wrong email or password." for it too. {{contact}}
