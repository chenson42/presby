A congregation can have a public website on PresbyPortal. Some of what it shows is controlled from the portal, and some is not. This page says which is which, so you know where to go to change something.

## What you change in PresbyPortal

- Colour, type pairing and logo: under "Branding". This needs a role the team sets up, and a Founding Administrator does not have it at first.
- Job openings: under "Job openings". A Founding Administrator can do this after turning on "Job openings" in "Features".
- Events on the public calendar: the event form, and "Public events calendar" in "Features". You turn the switch on, and adding events needs a role the team sets up. See [Events and the calendar](/help/events-and-the-calendar).
- Which people are named on the website: "Public listing" on "Officers", "Staff" and each group's roster. A Founding Administrator can do this.

## What whoever maintains your website changes

The text of your pages, their images and the website's menus are not edited in PresbyPortal. Neither is where the public calendar, the job openings and the list of people appear on a page. To change any of them, send a support ticket. See [Getting help](/help/getting-help).

This is why a setting in PresbyPortal can be on and nothing shows on your website: the website has to have a place for it.

## Colour, type pairing and logo

The "Branding" page sets your congregation's colour, a type pairing ("Classic", "Modern", "Warm" or "Contemporary"), an optional logo (PNG, JPEG or WEBP, up to 2 MB) and "Light mode only". Choose "Save brand" and the message is "Brand saved." The colour is used in the portal and on your public website.

A Founding Administrator does not have the role for this at first. The page says "You don't have permission to manage branding". The PresbyPortal team sets it up. {{contact}}

## Job openings

1. Open "Features" and turn on "Job openings". A "Job openings" tile appears under "Community".
2. Choose "New job opening". Enter a title and a location, and choose "Full-time", "Part-time" or "Intern". Pay is optional, and a closing date is optional. The web address is made of letters, numbers and dashes, and it cannot be changed after you publish.
3. Write the description. The buttons add headings, bold text, lists and links. Pasting from Word or Google Docs keeps headings, bold, italics, lists and links, so check the preview afterwards.
4. Choose "Save draft". The opening is not public yet.
5. Choose "Publish", then "Yes, publish". The message is "Published."

The "Job openings" page lists your openings as drafts, published and closed. "View on your website" opens a published one. People are asked to contact the church to apply, because there is no application form.

"Close" takes an opening off your website and out of the list. "Reopen" puts it back at the top of the list. A closing date is the last day you accept applications, and you can leave it blank to keep the opening until you close it.

A line under the heading says where your openings appear on your website. Your openings always have their own list page, and the line gives its address, which ends in /careers. If the line says no other page on your website shows them, ask whoever maintains your website to put them on a page.

## Naming people on the website

"Officers", "Staff" and each group's roster have a "Public listing" switch for each person. Turning it on asks you to confirm, because the person's name, role and, if there is one, photo become visible to anyone who visits your website, including search engines and archival crawlers. A photo appears only when a website photo has been chosen for the person, or their directory photo has been cleared for the website, and not if the person has hidden their photo. See [Photos of people](/help/photos-of-people). The list shows on your website only where whoever maintains it has put the list of people.

On a phone the "Public listing" column is not shown. Use a wider screen to change it.

## A web address of your own

A congregation can connect its own web address to its website. It is not something you set up yourself, and it is not on the Features page. The "Website domain" page says that if it is not on, you should ask PresbyPortal support to turn it on. {{contact}}
