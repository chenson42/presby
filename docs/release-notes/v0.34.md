## 0.34.0 — 2026-10-19

### Feature: Online giving through Stripe, a treasurer's gift desk, and year-end giving statements

**Value:** A congregation had no way to take a gift through the platform, record the checks and cash that come in on Sunday, show a member what they have given, or send a year-end statement. This release builds all of that, so a treasurer can run the congregation's giving in one place. It is built and tested end to end against recorded and signed Stripe test messages, but it has **never been run against a real Stripe account**, and nothing in it takes money until the platform operator sets up Stripe keys.

#### What's New

**Giving online.** A congregation connects its own Stripe account (the money goes straight to the congregation, not through PresbyPortal). A visitor to the congregation's website, or a signed-in member, can give once or monthly by card or bank transfer, and can choose to add the processing fee so the congregation receives the full amount. The donor is sent to Stripe's own payment page to enter card or bank details; those details never touch a PresbyPortal page. When giving is not switched on or not connected, the give page says online giving is not available right now and nothing is charged. If a congregation's existing website already has its own page at the "give" address, that page keeps working until the congregation's giving is switched on; the moment it is, the platform's give page takes its place (and the old page returns if giving is switched off), so the operator tells the congregation before switching it on.

**A treasurer's gift desk.** Under the congregation's admin area, a treasurer can:
- record gifts that did not come through Stripe (cash, check, stock), with a date, a fund and a donor, and void a recorded gift with a reason;
- set up funds (for example a general fund or a missions fund), retire one when it is no longer used, and see the gifts list with a spreadsheet download;
- see donors, correct a donor's contact details, and merge two records that turn out to be the same person;
- connect and check the congregation's Stripe account, set the fee-cover options, and see an overview with a "needs attention" count (for example a failed payment or an event that could not be processed);
- see each Stripe payout itemized and matched to the gifts it contains. Congregations should keep Stripe payouts on automatic; manual payouts are shown as not reconcilable;
- give other people in the congregation a recording-only or treasurer role, and take it away again.

Refunds, bank returns and disputes are never edits to a gift. Each is recorded as its own line against the gift, so the history stays true.

**Members see their own giving.** A member with a linked record sees their own gifts and their own year-end statements at **Giving** in the congregation portal, with a download for each statement. A statement that has been replaced by a correction is kept under "Earlier versions". Nobody can see another member's giving here, and a household relationship does not give anyone access to another person's gifts.

**Year-end statements.** A treasurer can preview and issue each donor's statement for a calendar year. Each statement is made once as a PDF and kept exactly as issued, with the congregation's name, address and EIN and the gifts it counts. If a gift is refunded or added afterward, the statement shows as out of date and a corrected version replaces it; the earlier one is kept. A donor with an email address can be sent a notice that a statement is available (the notice carries no amounts); a statement can also be printed in a batch for mailing, and the treasurer can record that it was mailed.

**Who can do what.** Three new permissions (all in the financial tier, and all requiring two-step sign-in): view, record, and manage. A new Treasurer role carries all three, and a Giving Recorder role carries record only. The first treasurer of a congregation is named by the PresbyPortal operator, not by the congregation, because nobody in a new congregation holds a giving permission yet. That treasurer can then give recording-only roles to others. A presbytery or synod cannot see a member congregation's giving, and cannot be granted treasury powers by an administrative commission.

#### What this release does not do, and what you should know before using it

- **Not tested against a real Stripe account.** Everything was checked against recorded and signed Stripe test messages. Before any congregation takes a real gift, the operator runs a test-mode walkthrough with a real Stripe test account, and several Stripe behaviors we could not confirm in advance are listed for that walkthrough (including how the congregation's payout status is reported, and which permissions a restricted key needs).
- **Nothing takes money without keys.** Until the platform's Stripe keys are set, the treasurer panel says online giving is not enabled on this installation, the give forms cannot be reached, and Stripe's messages are refused. The gift desk for cash, check and stock works without Stripe.
- **Year-end statements are switched off in production** until an accountant has reviewed the statement wording and four accounting questions (for example how a covered fee is counted). They work in development and testing. Real gifts recorded in 2026 cannot produce a statement until the operator turns statements on after that review, so a treasurer recording real gifts should be told.
- **Giving is off for every congregation until the operator turns it on, one congregation at a time.** It is available to congregations and new worshiping communities only. The old "Giving" placeholder tile that appeared for everyone is gone. Until the operator names a treasurer for a congregation, nobody there can open the giving admin pages.
- **No privacy notice page yet.** Donor names, addresses, emails and amounts are kept for at least seven years, and the platform's Stripe key can technically reach every connected congregation's account. A page that says so does not exist yet and is needed before real money.
- **Known gaps, all on the follow-up list:** the treasurer cannot list active monthly gifts (a donor changes or cancels one by contacting the congregation); a member who is hidden from the directory does not see their offline gifts in their own giving page (their statement still goes to the address on the donor record); a statement whose every gift was refunded cannot be corrected; a congregation still in Stripe test mode shows its members an empty history; the return page after a gift does not name the church; the member Statements page says statements follow the year's end even while the operator has statements switched off; and the "isn't taking online gifts" wording is not yet suited to presbytery and synod sites.

#### Permissions

| Permission | Required for |
|------------|--------------|
| `giving.view` (tier 2, two-step sign-in required) | gifts, donors, payouts and statements lists, downloads, CSV export |
| `giving.record` (tier 2, two-step sign-in required) | recording and voiding offline gifts, creating donors |
| `giving.manage` (tier 2, two-step sign-in required) | funds, Stripe connection, settings, issuing statements, granting giving-only roles |
| none (identity-based) | a member's own giving page and their own statement downloads |
| `ADMIN_ORGANIZATIONS` (platform) | naming a congregation's first treasurer, and the giving ingest-health page |

#### New Routes

- `GET /o/<slug>/giving` and `/giving/give` — a member's own gifts and statements; the signed-in give form
- `GET /o/<slug>/admin/giving` and its pages for gifts, funds, donors, connection, payouts, statements, settings and team
- `GET /site/<slug>/give` and `/give/return` — the public give page and the page Stripe returns the donor to
- `POST /api/webhooks/stripe` — Stripe's signed messages (refused with an error if the signing secret is not set)
- `GET /api/cron/giving` — the five-minute background worker
- `GET /admin/giving` — the operator's ingest-health view (counts only)
