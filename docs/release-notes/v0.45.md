## 0.45.0 — 2026-10-25

### Feature: deacon districts (parish management)

**Value:** A congregation can set up its deacon districts, put every household in one, see which households are not in a district yet, and let the Moderator of the Board of Deacons keep the assignments up to date, with every change recorded.

#### What's New

- **It is off until someone turns it on.** An administrator switches on "Parish management" under Features for their congregation. It is for congregations only, and no congregation has it on today.
- **Districts.** Administrators who manage officers can add, rename, archive and restore districts. A district cannot be deleted. An archived district keeps its households until they are moved, and nobody can be moved into it.
- **Putting a household in a district.** Pick a district and press Assign (or Move). Nothing happens when you only pick; the button does it. Each change shows a message with Undo for 10 seconds.
- **Households with no district.** A new "Unassigned" list sorts them into four groups: Members, Participants, Visitors and Others. Each group shows its count, and you can hide the groups you are not working on.
- **Emptying an archived district.** One confirmed action moves every household in an archived district to one other district, up to 100 households. A district with more than 100 households is emptied one household at a time. There is no Undo for the bulk move.
- **The Moderator seat.** The Officers page has a new office, "Moderator of the Board of Deacons". Recording someone in it, with a start date, lets that person assign households to districts once Parish management is on and they have a PresbyPortal account. It gives them nothing else, and ending the term ends it. Administrators who manage people can assign households too, with or without the seat.
- **Recording yourself.** A founding administrator may record themselves in the seat. That is allowed, and the Officers page marks it "Self-recorded". Record the seat only for the person the Board has chosen.
- **Private names stay private.** A household whose members hide their names shows "Private" to people who may not see hidden names. These pages show no addresses, phone numbers, emails or birthdays.
- **Every change is recorded** in the audit log, without household or person names. A household's district is overwritten when it moves, not kept as a dated history, and congregations cannot read the log in the app yet, so there is no "who moved this and when" screen.

#### A change to the Parishes tab count

The household count on the Parishes tab now counts every household assigned to the district. Before, it counted only households with someone shown in the directory. After this release some districts will show a higher number. The new count is the one a bulk move actually moves.

#### What stops someone gaining the seat's ability by another route

The ability to assign households can be given only by recording the seat. The database refuses to attach it to a named person, a commission or a delegation, to another role, or to a role changed to carry it. One gap is known and is not closed by this release: code that already has direct write access to the database could add a name to the seat's roster, or change how that roster is labelled, without a recorded term. No screen or action in the product does this, and the Session roster has the same gap. A later release (the Community work that adds migration 0066) closes both. Until then it is caught by review and the audit log, not prevented.

**For the operator: deploy order.** Take a snapshot of production, then apply migration `0068` to production first, then merge and deploy. The new code reads columns and tables that `0068` adds, so deploying first would break the directory's Parishes tab until the migration runs. The old code is expected to keep working against the new schema (not separately tested). Before applying, read the pre-ship counts in the work-log's "Production runbook for migration 0068": the directory and directory v2 flags must be on in production and every stop-block count must be 0. Applying gives every congregation an empty "Board of Deacons Moderator" group and role; nobody gains any ability until a term is recorded. Merging exposes nothing, because Parish management is off everywhere. There is no what's-new post yet: publish one when the first congregation turns it on, for the Moderator rather than for members.

**Known gaps.** A household with no living, current person and no district is not reachable from any screen. A first-day administrator is not pointed to Parish management from the portal home or the admin page (follow-up N1), and on a new congregation the Unassigned list reads "Every household is in a district." before any household exists (N3). Small touch targets remain on the Undo message and a few links at phone width (N2).

**Technical:** migration `0068_presby_parishes` and DECISION-242 to DECISION-245. `org_units` gains `archived_at`, a validated `unit_type` and name-shape CHECK, a unique active-name index and a `households (organization_id, org_unit_id)` index; a guard trigger on `households` and open `officer_terms` refuses an archived, campus or missing district; DELETE on `org_units` is revoked and guarded on the owner path. The seat is the office `deacons_moderator`, a derived `roster` group of the same key and a protected `deacons_moderator` role carrying only `parishes.assign`, made seat-only by `presby_seat_key_group` and triggers on `app_role_permissions`, `role_grants`, `administrative_commissions` and `org_delegations`. `presby_sync_derived_group()` is re-created `SECURITY DEFINER` with a pinned `search_path` so 0066 applies in either order. `src/lib/parishes.ts` is the only writer of `org_units` and `households.org_unit_id`; moves are conditional updates under RLS with `org_units` locked before `households`, and the audit rows are written in the same transaction.
