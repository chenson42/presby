## 0.29.0 — 2026-10-05

### A platform operator can hand a newly created organization to its first real administrator

**Background:** Until now a freshly created presbytery or congregation had nobody inside it who could do anything. Every permission on the platform is granted by someone who already holds it at that organization, so a brand-new organization had no first person to start the chain — every presbytery feature built this year was reachable only in test data. This release closes that gap, which is the last thing standing between the presbytery portal and a real presbytery being onboarded.

**Changes:**
- On an organization's page in the platform admin area, an operator can now designate a **founding administrator** by typing the email address of someone who has already signed up. A confirmation names the organization, the person, and exactly what they will be able to do before anything is written.
- The founding administrator receives a deliberately bounded set of abilities: managing people, roles and role assignments, groups, officers, staff, feature toggles and support tickets for their own organization (plus congregation oversight for a presbytery), together with the organization's own constitutional clerk office (stated clerk for a presbytery or congregation). They receive nothing from the pastoral, demographic or medical tiers, and nothing lets a platform operator act inside the organization afterwards — every later grant flows through the organization's own role administration.
- The handoff is one-time by construction: the control disappears as soon as anyone at the organization can manage roles, and reappears only if that count ever falls back to zero, which doubles as the recovery path if an organization locks itself out.
- A person who already belongs to a different organization, whose account is inactive, or whose relationship with this organization has ended is refused with a plain sentence saying why. The act is recorded in the audit log.
- Known limits, stated plainly: the person must have an account first (an invitation flow for someone who has not signed up yet is a later release); and a congregation's founding administrator can add staff and delegate roles but cannot yet enrol a member on the roll, because roll proposals and approvals are not carried by any template on the platform today. That does not affect a presbytery, which is what this release is for, and it is tracked for the first congregation's go-live.
