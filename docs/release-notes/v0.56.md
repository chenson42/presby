## 0.56.0 — 2026-11-11

### Feature: Spot the scam

**Value:** Your organization can now give its people a short, plain practice in telling genuine messages from scams, including the newest kind: a voicemail in a loved one's cloned voice. It is off until an administrator turns it on under "Features". Every kind of organization can use it: congregations, new worshiping communities, presbyteries and synods.

#### What's New

**The practice.** Seven made-up emails, texts and voicemails, five scams and two genuine. After each one, the page says "Right." or "Not quite." and lists what gave the message away. The seventh is a voicemail from a "grandchild in trouble", the scam that now uses a cloned voice. The messages all come from one made-up church, Thistledown Presbyterian Church. None of the messages has a link or a phone number. Your answers are never sent or saved: reloading or leaving the page starts over.

**Six rules to keep.** Under the practice is a short list of six rules. It can be printed without the practice, for a bulletin or a refrigerator door, using your browser's Print command.

**Where people find it.**
- **Signed in:** a new "Learn" area, with a "Spot the scam" tile on your organization's page and "Learn" in the menu. The page also shows the leader the public address to share, once your public website is published.
- **On your public website:** its own page at an address ending in `/learn/spot-the-scam`. Anyone can open it without signing in, and search engines may list it.
- **Inside a website page:** whoever maintains your website can also place it on any page of the site.

**Turning it on and off.** An administrator turns it on under "Features". A new "Learn" switch under "Ministry areas" shows or hides the whole area, now and as more learning pages arrive. Turning Spot the scam off, or Learn off, hides it everywhere for your organization: the tile, the menu entry, the public page (which then reads as a page that does not exist) and the website slot. Nothing is stored, so nothing is lost.

**Good to know.**
- The messages are the same for every organization. There is no score kept, no certificate and no report of who took part.
- On an iPhone, a sender's address (for example an email-shaped one) inside a practice that your website maintainer has placed on one of your own website pages may show as a tappable link, because that page's settings belong to your website, not to PresbyPortal. The practice's own public page and the signed-in page tell the phone not to do this. This has not yet been checked on a real iPhone.
- There is no switch to turn Spot the scam off for every organization at once. Each organization turns it off for itself.
- The picture on the "Turning features on and off" help page still shows six "Ministry areas" switches. The page's text and a note under the picture name the new "Learn" switch; the picture will be retaken.
- With JavaScript turned off, the page shows the first message and the six rules, and says the practice needs JavaScript.

**Help.** New help page: "Spot the scam". "Turning features on and off", "Your congregation's website", "Your first day as an administrator" and "What PresbyPortal does today" now mention it.

**For the operator.**
- Migration `0087` must be applied to production **before** this deploys (`docs/deployment.md` "Learn (0087)"). It adds no table and changes nothing for any organization. If the code deploys first, every live website's `/learn/spot-the-scam` page shows an "isn't available right now" notice instead of a plain not-found page, and the Learn switch cannot be saved on the Features page.
- Confirm in production that the platform flags `org_portal.features`, `org_portal.feature_categories`, `org_portal.home_v2` and `org_portal.chrome_v2` are on. The help page promises a Features switch, a Learn switch, a tile and a menu entry, and each needs one of them; an administrator without it will not see what the page describes.
- Before the synod's own website is rebuilt: if its pages include one at `/learn/spot-the-scam`, that page is shown instead of the platform's practice, and the portal's leader panel will still say the platform's practice is live (tracked in `docs/TODO.md`).
