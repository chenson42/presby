This page is for when a page, a button or a whole congregation is missing or does not open. Find the heading that matches what you see.

## Not connected to a congregation

After you sign in, PresbyPortal opens your congregation if you belong to one. If you belong to none, it opens a page headed with one of these:

- "You're not connected to a congregation yet". You are signed in, but no church, presbytery or synod has added you to its records.
- "Your organization is being set up", or "Your organizations are being set up". The page names each one that is not ready yet.
- "Your congregation isn't on PresbyPortal yet". The page names your congregation and says it is not set up on PresbyPortal yet.
- "Your access has ended". The page names the congregation and the date your access ended.

What to do: ask your church administrator or the person who invited you. If you have an invitation email, open the link in it. You may be signed in with a different email address from the one your congregation has for you. Open the account menu at the top right to see which address you are using.

## Access pending

The "Access pending" page says "Your account exists, but doesn't yet have permission for this area. Ask an administrator to grant you the right role."

You see it when you open one of the PresbyPortal team's own staff pages with an account that is not on the team. Your congregation's pages are not in that area. Select "Back to home" to leave.

## A page or button is missing

PresbyPortal shows some things only when they apply to your congregation, and some pages only to people with the right role. What you see tells you which it is.

| What you see | What it means | Who can fix it |
|---|---|---|
| A tile or menu entry you expect is not there, for example "Calendar" or "Job openings". | The feature is turned off for your congregation, or it is for a different kind of organization. | An administrator can turn it on in "Features". A feature made for a different kind of organization cannot be turned on. |
| The page opens and says it "isn't turned on" for your congregation. | The feature is turned off for your congregation. | An administrator can turn it on in "Features". |
| The page opens and says "You don't have permission to" do something. | Your role does not include it. The message names your stated clerk or another administrator as the person to ask. | An administrator can give you a role in "Roles". |
| A feature you cannot find in "Features", for example "Website domain". | The PresbyPortal team turns it on for you. | {{contact}} |

For how an administrator turns features on and off, see [Turning features on and off](/help/turning-features-on-and-off).

A few tiles open a page that only describes an idea and has nothing to use. They are not features you can turn on.

## A congregation says you do not have access

If you open a congregation you do not belong to, you see "You don't have access to" and its name. The message is the same whether or not the congregation uses PresbyPortal, so it tells you nothing about that.

If you used to belong, the page says your access ended, with the date, and tells you to ask an administrator there to restore it.

If you should have access, ask an administrator at that congregation to add you. They need the email address you signed in with. Select "Back to your organizations" to go to your home page.
