PresbyPortal is a website that Presbyterian congregations use to keep their records in one place. Each congregation's records can be seen only by the people who have access to that congregation. PresbyPortal is in early release, so some screens change as it grows.

## What a congregation can do today

These are things an administrator or a member can do now. Each one needs the right role, and some start turned off until an administrator turns them on.

- **Keep a list of its people.** An administrator adds people and sees them as members, participants, visitors, inactive people, staff or former members. A new member waits for the Session to approve before joining the roll.
- **Share a directory.** People who are connected to the congregation can search a directory, read a household's page and see each person's contact details, except the details a person has marked private.
- **Record officers.** An administrator records officer terms. The Session and the deacons' rosters are built from those terms.
- **Decide who can do what.** Roles decide what each person can do. An administrator gives roles and takes them away.
- **Keep groups.** An administrator keeps committees, small groups, choirs and teams.
- **Record staff.** An administrator records paid, non-ordained staff positions.
- **Show a calendar.** Members can read the congregation's calendar.
- **Post job openings.** An administrator writes and manages job openings for the congregation's website.
- **Set the congregation's look.** Someone with the right role chooses the colour, type pairing and logo.
- **Ask for help.** Church administrators file tickets and anyone can send feedback. See [Getting help](/help/getting-help).

## Who it is for

PresbyPortal is written for congregations. Presbyteries and synods have their own pages in it, and this help does not describe them.

## What it does not do

PresbyPortal does not do everything a congregation needs. [Things PresbyPortal does not do yet](/help/planned) lists some of what is missing.
