## 0.51.0 — 2026-10-31

### Feature: Reimbursements

**Value:** A member who bought something for the church, or a leader who needs a vendor paid, had no way to ask inside PresbyPortal. Requests travelled by paper, email and memory, so nobody could see what was waiting, who had said yes, or whether it had been paid. Now the request, the approval and the payment are one record that a phone can start and everyone involved can follow.

#### What's New

**Reimbursements.** Members can now ask your church to pay them back for something they bought for it, or ask the church to pay a vendor, from a phone, with photos or PDFs of the receipt. The chair or leader of the group the cost is charged to approves the request, and the treasurer records the payment. No one can approve their own request, and the person who approved it can't also record the payment. If your church has only one person who can record payments, that person may record a payment on their own request, and the record shows that they did. PresbyPortal keeps the record; it doesn't send money or collect bank details. Photos are re-saved without their hidden location data. PDFs are kept exactly as uploaded, and PresbyPortal does not scan files for viruses. Nothing in the app deletes a request: a mistake is corrected by adding a new entry, and turning the feature off only hides the pages. Reimbursements are off until an administrator turns them on under Features. A treasurer designated through PresbyPortal now also sees and pays these requests and chooses which groups can receive them; existing treasurers are updated automatically. Requests charged to the Session or the Board of Deacons go to the treasurer for approval for now, and a church can't narrow who may file other than by turning the feature off. Not in this release: reminders for waiting approvals, importing earlier requests, and entries in a church ledger.

- **Filing.** One form, one column on a phone: pay me back, or pay a vendor. Up to five receipts of up to 4 MB each, or a box to say the receipt can't be attached (the treasurer decides whether that is acceptable before paying). A request can be saved as a draft, sent back for changes and sent again, or withdrawn.
- **Approving.** The chair or leader of the group gets an email with a link and nothing else: the email carries no amount, name or purpose. The page shows the request, asks for confirmation with the amount and the person named, and offers Approve or Send back.
- **Paying.** The treasurer sees a queue by status, records the payment (the date, how it was paid and a reference such as a check number), and can reverse it with a reason. A reversal keeps both entries visible. The queue exports to CSV; every export is recorded.
- **The treasurer's team page.** A treasurer can set up and give the Treasurer or the Reimbursement Clerk role (the clerk can file on someone else's behalf) without asking PresbyPortal.
- **A printed form.** A request can be printed as a one-page form from its page.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `reimbursement.view` | See every request and the queue, export the CSV (two-factor required) |
| `reimbursement.pay` | Record and reverse payments, waive a receipt, send back, void, approve as treasurer when no seat holder exists (two-factor required) |
| `reimbursement.manage` | Choose which groups can receive requests, manage categories, and set up the treasury team (two-factor required) |
| `reimbursement.request_for_others` | File a request on someone else's behalf |

There is no key for filing or approving. Filing is open to a member on the roll, a regular attender or staff. Approving belongs to the chair or leader of the charged group, checked each time. Treasurers designated through PresbyPortal receive all four keys (existing ones through migration `0074`, later ones when designated); no other role gains a key.

#### New Routes

- `GET /o/<slug>/reimbursements`, `/new`, `/<id>`, `/<id>/edit` — the member's list, form, request and edit pages
- `POST /o/<slug>/reimbursements/<id>/receipts` and `GET …/receipts/<attachmentId>` — receipt upload and download, only through the request
- `GET /o/<slug>/reimbursements/<id>/print` — the one-page form
- `GET /o/<slug>/admin/reimbursements`, `/<id>`, `/settings`, `/team` — the treasurer's queue, request, settings and team pages
- `GET /o/<slug>/admin/reimbursements/export` — CSV, capped at 20,000 rows

**For the operator.** This release has two migrations, `0073` and `0074`, and both must be applied to production **before** the toggle is turned on for any congregation. The code is safe to deploy first: nothing reads the new tables until `org_portal.reimbursement` is on for an organization.
1. Confirm `0072` is live (it shipped in 0.46.1; `0073` depends on the constraint it creates): `select 1 from pg_constraint where conname = 'blob_assets_id_org_audience_key'` returns a row.
2. Snapshot production, as the earlier runbooks do.
3. Apply, in order, on the direct (non-pooled) endpoint: `psql "$MIGRATE_DATABASE_URL" -1 -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 -f drizzle/0073_presby_reimbursements.sql`, then `psql "$MIGRATE_DATABASE_URL" -1 -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 -f drizzle/0074_presby_reimbursement_permissions.sql`. Each is safe to repeat. `0080` and `0082` are already applied, so these two go in after higher numbers; they do not depend on either.
4. `0074`'s backfill gives the four keys to every treasurer role that a designation created. When last counted (2026-10-26) production had one.
5. **EC42, the `sharp` check, is the one thing no test can prove.** The image library that strips location data must load inside a Vercel function. Prove it with one real receipt upload and a download in production, with `org_portal.reimbursement` on for a fixture or test congregation only. Until that works, do not turn it on for a real congregation. If it fails, receipts are blocked and the work returns to design.
6. Also before any real congregation: the walk on `development` (the Phase 6 N1 line in `docs/TODO.md`). `development` now carries `0073` and `0074`, and the seed has a new linked fixture user, `p73.rowan@example.invalid` (integration commit `37decc2`), for the filing walk. The Features page needs the platform flag `org_portal.features`; it is seeded off, so confirm it is on in the environment you walk.

**Known limits, recorded and accepted on 2026-10-26.** The database refuses to delete a request or a payment, but the database owner credential, which the app never uses, could still empty one of the six tables or switch its guards off; that is the same posture as giving (DECISION-267). Ending a treasurer's role on one team page can leave the other team page without a manager; the platform operator can repair it, and the action is recorded (DECISION-269). Nothing scans receipts for viruses, and an approving chair or leader is not asked for two-factor (the independent payer is the control).

**Technical:** DECISION-267 (one module, state machine and history in the database, ledger-ready insert-only payments), DECISION-268 (approval is a live seat check; separation of duties by trigger), DECISION-269 (eligibility check instead of a filing key, the keys, bindings, backfill and delegation, residual 12), DECISION-270 (private receipts, `sharp`, 4 MB, upload route; partly superseded by DECISION-277), DECISION-271 (shared CSV writer, `check:treasury-boundaries`, the tenth tripwire, person-addressed notices, bare-path links) and DECISION-299 (treasurer-tree refusals, the queue's group filter); findings F296, F299 to F301 and F321 to F323 (`docs/schema-design-2.md` section 2am); migrations `0073_presby_reimbursements.sql` (six tables, FORCE RLS, 30 definers, triggers) and `0074_presby_reimbursement_permissions.sql` (keys, template bindings, backfill); a direct `sharp@0.35.5` dependency; `src/lib/reimbursement/`, `src/lib/treasury/`.
