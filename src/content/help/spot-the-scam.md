Spot the scam is a short practice in telling genuine messages from scams. It shows seven made-up messages, among them emails, a text and a voicemail, and after each one it says what gave it away. Your answers are never sent or saved.

## Who it is for

Anyone. It is written for the people who read an organization's email and answer its phone: members, officers, treasurers and staff. It works well together, on a screen after worship or at a meeting. It is for any kind of organization, not only congregations.

## Turning it on

An administrator turns it on under "Features", where it is called "Spot the scam". It is off until they do. See [Turning features on and off](/help/turning-features-on-and-off).

When it is on:

- People who sign in find a "Spot the scam" tile under "Learn" on the organization's page, and "Learn" in the menu.
- Once the public website is published, the practice is also on the website, at an address that ends in /learn/spot-the-scam. Anyone can open it without signing in.
- Whoever maintains the website can also place it on a page of the website. See [Your congregation's website](/help/your-congregations-website).

If "Learn" is turned off under "Ministry areas" in "Features", the practice stays hidden even when "Spot the scam" is on. While it is hidden, the portal page says "Spot the scam isn't turned on for" the organization's name, and the website address says the page does not exist.

## Doing the practice

1. Read the message. It is made up, and it comes from a made-up church, Thistledown Presbyterian Church.
2. Choose "Looks genuine" or "Something's off".
3. The page says "Right." or "Not quite." and lists what to notice. Choose "Next message".
4. After the last message, "See your result" shows how many you got right out of seven. Choose "Practice again" to go through them once more.

None of the messages contains a link or a phone number. Your answers stay on the page, so reloading or leaving the page starts over.

## The six rules

Under the practice, the page lists "The six rules to keep". Use your browser's Print command to print them without the practice, for a bulletin or a refrigerator door. The rest of the page, such as the website's menu, may print too.

## If the practice does not appear

If the page says "This practice needs JavaScript", your browser has JavaScript turned off. The six rules are still on the page.

## What is not here

The messages are the same for every organization. There is no score kept, no certificate and no report of who has taken part. The practice does not check messages you have received.
