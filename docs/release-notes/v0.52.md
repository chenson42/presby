## 0.52.0 — 2026-10-31

### Feature: Photos of people

**Value:** A congregation's directory showed initials, and its public staff and committee pages had no faces, because there was no way to attach a picture to a person. Staff who manage people can now add photos, choose which one members see and which one the public website shows, and take either down, from one screen on a phone.

#### What's New

**Photos of people.** A congregation can now turn on "Photos of people". It is off until someone who manages the congregation's features switches it on. Staff who manage people can then add up to ten photos to a person's record, choose the photo members see in the directory, and choose the photo shown on the public website for people who are listed there. Photos are kept separately for each congregation, so a person who belongs to two congregations has their photos set up in each. A directory photo can also appear on the public website when no separate website photo is chosen, but only if the staff member who chose it ticked the box saying the person agreed. That box is a staff member's recorded statement, not a signed consent from the person, and choosing a website photo needs the same tick. A photo appears on the website only while the person is listed there (listing is still set separately under Staff, Officers or Groups) and the website is published. Photos are resized when they are added (JPEG, PNG or WebP) and stored without location information. Photos taken directly with an iPhone camera have not been checked yet; if one is refused, choose it from the photo library or convert it to JPEG, as the message says. A person who appears to be under 18, judged from a recorded birth date, a birth year that could mean under 18, or a child marking in their household, has no photo used in the directory or on the website; someone with no birth date and no child marking is treated as an adult, so staff should check before adding. If a person's photo has been marked hidden, it is not shown on the public website for any congregation they belong to, and not in the directory of the congregation where it was hidden. Members cannot change that setting themselves yet, so for now staff removing a photo is how one comes down. Removing a photo, or turning "Photos of people" off, stops it being used on every screen, including the public website, and turning the setting back on restores what was chosen. Neither erases the stored image file, browsers that already saved a photo can keep showing it for up to a day, and copies kept by search engines or web archives cannot be recalled. A photo that is both the directory photo and cleared for the website stays on the website when you stop using it as the website photo; stop using it in the directory, then choose it again without ticking the box. This release does not include photos of children, household photos, uploading in bulk, or members adding their own photo.

- **Where it lives.** On a person's Edit page, a "Photos" link opens a screen with their photos as tiles. Each tile shows what it is used for ("Directory", "Website", "Directory, cleared for website") and has buttons to use it in the directory, use it on the website, stop using it, or remove it. Under "Where photos show" the screen says, in plain words, what members and the website will see for this person.
- **The directory.** A card shows the person's directory photo as a small square, or initials when there is none or the photo may not be shown.
- **The public website.** The staff, officer and committee lists on a congregation's site show the website photo (or the cleared directory photo) for people who are listed. A person with no photo shows as before.
- **Honest dialogs.** Each choice asks first and says what will change. The sentences are worked out from what the system would do before and after the click, not written by hand per case, and tests check every sentence against the same rules the database uses.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `people.manage` | Add, choose and remove a person's photos (existing key; no new key) |
| `org_features.manage` | Turn "Photos of people" on or off under Features (existing key) |

The per-congregation toggle is `org_portal.person_photos`. There is no new platform flag. The Photos link sits on the Edit person page, which needs the Community feature on as well.

#### New Routes

- `GET /o/<slug>/admin/community/<id>/edit/photos` — the Photos screen for one person (add, choose, stop, remove)
- The existing public asset address on a congregation's site (`/site/<slug>/assets/<key>`) now also serves a person's website photo, only while a roster lists that person.

**Help:** `/help/photos-of-people` is new and published (verified against this release); `/help/using-the-directory` and `/help/your-congregations-website` are edited to describe photos.

**For the operator.** This release has one migration, `0078`, and it must be applied to production **before** the code is promoted. Old code against the new schema is harmless, but new code against the old schema fails. The full runbook, with the checks, is `docs/deployment.md` "Pre-deploy runbook: 0078 (person photos), then the code".
1. Order: `0072` (already live), `0073` and `0074` (release 0.51.0), then `0078`. `0078` refuses and rolls back whole if `0072` is missing.
2. Before applying, confirm no person has a photo key (`select count(*) filter (where photo_key is not null), count(*) filter (where photo_updated_at is not null) from people` returns `0` and `0`). If either is not zero, stop and bring it back; do not null the values to get past the check.
3. Rehearse on a throwaway Neon branch made from production, snapshot production, then apply on the direct (non-pooled) endpoint: `psql "$MIGRATE_DATABASE_URL" -1 -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 -f drizzle/0078_presby_person_photos.sql`. Run the six checks V1 to V6 in the runbook, then promote the code. `development` already carries `0078`.
4. **Never re-apply `0072` after `0078`.** It would silently put back the old roster functions and every person photo would disappear from the public site. If `0072` ever has to be re-run, re-apply `0078` straight after it.
5. `0079`, which drops the old `people.photo_key` columns, is a later release, never the same one.
6. **Two conditions before the toggle is turned on for any real congregation** (it is off everywhere at deploy). First, one real photo added on a Vercel-built deployment, to a throwaway adult on a test congregation: the image library has been checked only on a laptop, and the preview build being green is a merge condition, not this proof. Receipts use the same pinned library but need their own real upload (release 0.51.0). Second, your real-iPhone check at 360px: a 48-megapixel HEIC taken with the camera, and a 12-megapixel JPEG chosen from the library, each added through "Add a photo". Do not tell any congregation that iPhone camera photos work until both pass.
7. Not decided yet: whether the first customer's leadership and committee pages move onto the live rosters (the email and phone lines on those pages would be lost). Until you decide, those pages stay hand-written.

**Known limits, recorded and accepted.** A person with no birth date and no child marking counts as an adult. Nobody can set a person's "hide my photo" yet, and a hide set at another congregation is not visible on this page until a photo is chosen, so the page may offer a choice that the person's hide will then block; the page shows the hide once a choice has been made. The "OK for the public website" tick is a staff statement, not the person's consent. A photo's stored bytes live in the database, so growth is watched (the revisit points are 100 MB across the platform or 10% of the database, and 500 KB for a 25-card directory page). A listed person's website photo can still be fetched by its exact link while the site's staff or committee list is switched off; that was stated in 0.46.1 and is unchanged.
