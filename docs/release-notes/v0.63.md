## 0.63.0 — 2026-11-19

### Feature: Presbytery committees

**Value:** A presbytery can keep its committees in one place: which are required, standing or ad hoc, who serves on each in which role, and since when. That history stays, so "who chaired this in 2025" has an answer.

#### What's New

- **Committees & Commissions is no longer "coming soon".** At a presbytery, the tile opens a list of committees grouped as Required, Standing and Ad hoc. Each shows its chair, how many people serve now, and when it meets.
- **Start and edit a committee.** It has a name, a kind, and optionally a purpose and a meeting time. Two active committees can't share a name.
- **Members with dated roles.** The roles are Chair, Vice chair, Clerk, Secretary, Ex officio and Member.
  - A term can start later, so you can plan an appointment ahead.
  - A term can have a set end date.
  - **Change role** ends the old role and starts the new one on the same day, so there is no gap. Both stay in the history.
  - **End term** moves a person to Past members. Nothing is deleted.
- **Titles give no access.** If a whole committee carries a role, the page says so, because adding someone then gives them that role's access.
- **Dissolve a committee.** Every term ends on that date and the committee moves to "Dissolved committees". It can't be undone from the page.
- **People come from the presbytery's own records.** A ruling elder from a congregation is added under Community first, as a visitor or participant.
  - A member who has left the presbytery is marked "No longer connected to this presbytery".
  - A member recorded as deceased is marked "Recorded as deceased".
- **Public website committee list** (where a website has one and a member is opted in):
  - It now shows every role title.
  - It shows people only while their term is under way.
  - It no longer shows anyone who has left the presbytery, has died, or is on a dissolved committee.
  - The list changes at midnight UTC, so it can be up to a day early or late.
- **Committee groups move off the Groups screen at a presbytery.** That screen now points to Committees & Commissions. Congregations are unchanged.
- **Not yet:** administrative commissions. The page says so. Recording them comes in the next release.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `groups.manage` (existing; in the presbytery founding-administrator bundle) | Everything on Committees & Commissions |

No new permission and no new flag. The existing platform flag `org_portal.committees` is on in production, so the page goes live with this release. Switching it off hides the pages and refuses every change; nothing is deleted.

#### Database

Migration 0090 is applied to production before the code:
- a closed list of seven roles;
- a committee's label and dissolved date;
- the tables for the next release's commission records, which stay empty for now;
- the public committee list's corrected rules.

A commission record gives no one access to a congregation. Nothing in this release writes the separate tables that could.

#### New Routes

- `/o/<slug>/admin/committees`: the list, with `new`, a committee page, `edit`, `members/new` and `members/<id>/role`.

**Help:** /help/presbytery-committees (new; replaces the "Presbytery committees and commissions" placeholder), /help/roles-who-can-do-what, /help/why-cant-i-see-this, /help/privacy-policy.
