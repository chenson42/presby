Some accounts are asked for a six-digit code every time they sign in, and some are not. This page says which, and why. The code comes from an authenticator app on your phone. See [Using your code to sign in](/help/using-your-code).

## The rule

You are asked for a code if any one of these is true:

- your own account setting requires it;
- you hold a role that requires it;
- a congregation you belong to requires it for everyone it has.

If any one applies, you are asked. PresbyPortal decides this when you sign in, before you choose a congregation, so a requirement from one congregation applies to your whole sign-in.

If none applies, you are not asked. A new account is not asked just because it is new. Accounts that existed before keep the setting they had.

## Your own account setting

Each account has its own setting. The PresbyPortal team can switch it on or off on a person's account. You cannot change it yourself.

People who administer PresbyPortal itself are asked from the moment they are given that role. Their own setting can be turned off later, and they are then asked only if a role or a congregation requires it.

## Your role

These roles require a code:

- a role that lets you see gifts, record gifts or manage giving, such as a treasurer;
- a role that lets you create or change roles;
- a role that lets you give roles to other people;
- a role that lets you manage your congregation's website addresses.

A congregation's first administrator holds roles that create roles, give roles and manage website addresses, so a first administrator is asked. Roles that cover none of these, such as a role for recording people or one for events, do not require a code on their own.

If you are given one of these roles while you are signed in, you are asked the next time you sign in.

## Your congregation's policy

A congregation can require a code from everyone it has. The PresbyPortal team sets this for the congregation. If it is on, every person who belongs to that congregation is asked.

## If you set up two-factor sign-in on your own

You can set up two-factor sign-in even when nothing requires it. Doing so does not start asking you for a code when you sign in. You are asked only when one of the reasons above applies.

## The whole installation

The PresbyPortal team can switch the requirement off for everyone. While it is off, nobody is asked, whatever else is true.

## If you are asked and have not set it up

PresbyPortal takes you to the set-up page when you sign in and does not open a congregation's pages until you have linked your authenticator app. See [Setting up two-factor sign-in](/help/setting-up-two-factor). If you are a new first administrator or treasurer, [Accepting your invitation](/help/accepting-your-invitation) walks through the whole path.
