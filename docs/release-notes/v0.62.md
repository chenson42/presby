## 0.62.0 — 2026-11-18

### Feature: Building photos and two new reports for congregation records

**Value:** A presbytery can keep pictures of each congregation's buildings with its records, and pull the buildings, insurance and loans across all its congregations into one list or spreadsheet, instead of opening each record.

#### What's New

- **Building photos.** On a congregation's record, each building gains a Photos page.
  - Choose a picture from your phone's photo library or camera, add a caption, and mark one as the "Cover".
  - A building keeps up to 10 photos. The first photo is the cover until you choose another.
  - The picture is shrunk on your device first and saved without its location or camera details.
  - Photos are private to the presbytery's staff and are never published.
- **Removing a photo** takes it out of view but does not erase it: the picture file stays in storage and can't be erased from here. Rarely, if a photo fails to save partway, a stray file can be left in storage; it is not shown anywhere.
- **Two new reports** join the four on the presbytery's reports page:
  - **Buildings:** every building in use across the member congregations, primary first. A congregation with none reads "None in use" (all retired) or "Not recorded".
  - **Insurance and loans:** each congregation's newest insurance year, open loans with totals, guarantees and appraised value. A missing figure is counted as "not recorded", never shown as $0.
- **Spreadsheet downloads (CSV).** Both reports have one, and each download is recorded in the audit trail.
  - The Buildings file leaves out street addresses and notes.
  - The Insurance-and-loans file includes each loan's guarantee, and leaves out street addresses, policy numbers, lenders and notes.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `congregation_oversight.manage` (existing) | Building photos; the Buildings report and its download |
| `congregation_oversight.finance` (existing since 0.61.0, two-factor) | The Insurance-and-loans report and its download |

No new permission, flag or migration.

#### New Routes

- `/o/<slug>/admin/oversight/<congregation>/buildings/<building>/photos`: a building's photos.
- `/o/<slug>/admin/reports/buildings` and `/o/<slug>/admin/reports/insurance-and-loans`, each with an `/export` CSV download.

Like the rest of congregation records, these are reachable once the PresbyPortal team switches Congregation Oversight on.

**Help:** /help/congregation-records (Photos; Reports from these records), /help/presbytery-reports (the two new reports), /help/privacy-policy (building photos), and the presbytery-tools placeholder (six reports).
