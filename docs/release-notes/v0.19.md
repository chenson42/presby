## 0.19.0 — 2026-08-27

### Feature: Domain-organized portal and the product map

**Value:** The portal's flat tile list and nav row couldn't scale to the full product. Functionality is now organized into named domains, and every planned area of the product appears as an honest "coming soon" page — so the shape of the final product is visible in the portal today.

#### What's New

- The portal home and the Administration hub group their tiles under labeled domain sections (People & Membership, Worship & Events, Giving & Finance, Governance & Courts, Reports & Insights, Communications, Administration).
- The navigation row now shows one entry per domain instead of one per tool, linking to that domain's section on the portal home — it stays compact no matter how many tools a domain gains.
- Seven planned areas — giving & finance, worship planning, insights, communications, and the presbytery's committees, oversight, and reports — have their own pages describing what's coming, each behind its own feature flag (currently on in development so the roadmap is visible; they will be turned off before launch).
- "Give feedback" is no longer a tile or menu entry: it now lives in your account menu (top-right) and as the dismissible feedback prompt at the bottom of the portal home — the same prompt the platform home page uses, with the same snooze and opt-out choices.
- Presbytery-only areas appear only at presbytery organizations, using the same organization-type scoping the Credentials tool introduced.

#### Flags

| Flag | Gates |
|------|-------|
| `org_portal.giving`, `org_portal.worship`, `org_portal.insights`, `org_portal.communications` | Coming-soon areas for every organization |
| `org_portal.committees`, `org_portal.oversight`, `org_portal.reports` | Presbytery-only coming-soon areas |

#### New Routes

- `GET /o/<slug>/admin/{giving,worship,insights,communications,committees,oversight,reports}` — the coming-soon pages

### Enhancement: Modernized buttons

**Value:** Buttons were flat, and on branded organizations the color math could pick black text on a mid-tone brand color — harsh and dated.

**Changes:**
- On branded organizations, action buttons now use a deepened shade of the congregation's own color with white text that always meets the accessibility contrast floor (in light mode; dark mode computes its own correct pairing).
- Buttons gained a subtle shadow and hover depth; segmented button groups keep their connected look.
- Disabled buttons now render in a neutral muted style that clearly reads as inactive, instead of a washed-out version of the fill color.

---

### Enhancement: Larger control type

**Value:** The audience skews older; 14-point control text was the floor of comfortable.

**Changes:**
- Every text input, textarea, and dropdown now reads at 16px at every screen size (16px inputs also stop iPhone Safari's auto-zoom on focus).
- Buttons are 16px semibold across the app.
- The home page's find-a-person search box now uses the same input component as every other search field — the last rendering difference between search bars is gone.

---

### Defect Fix: Browser autofill turned inputs gray

**Problem:** In Chrome-family browsers (including Arc), a search box the browser had autofilled rendered with a gray-blue tint that didn't match the identical empty field next to it.

**Root Cause:** Chromium paints autofilled fields with its own overlay color that ordinary page styling cannot override.

**Fix:** The app now counter-paints autofilled fields with its own background token, so an autofilled field looks identical to an empty one.

**Testing:**
- [x] Verified page styling was already consistent in both Chromium and WebKit before the fix (the tint only appears on browser-autofilled fields)
- [x] Operator confirmation on the reporting browser (Arc)
