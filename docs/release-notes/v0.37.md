## 0.37.1 — 2026-10-22

### Polish: no developer hint on the sign-in page

**Value:** The sign-in page used to end with "First time? Run `npm run db:seed` to provision the seeded admin user." That line was meant for developers setting up the project on their own computer, but it also showed on the live site whenever email sign-in was on. It is gone. Nothing else on the page changes.

Also in this release, for the operator: email delivery status can now be reported back to the platform (the Resend webhook secret is set in production), so after this deploy a sent email, including an invitation, can move from "Sent" to "Delivered" or "Bounced" once the webhook in Resend is pointed at the platform.

## 0.37.0 — 2026-10-21

### Feature: Invite a congregation's first administrator and treasurer by email

**Value:** Until now, the platform operator could name a congregation's founding administrator or first treasurer only if that person already had a PresbyPortal account. Otherwise the page said "the account did not exist yet" and the person had to sign up first. During production testing this blocked onboarding the first customer. Now the operator types the person's email address, the platform sends a one-time invitation link, and the person either creates an account or signs in to the one they have. They receive the access only after they accept.

#### What's New

**Sending an invitation.** On an organization's page under **Admin > Organizations**, the founding-administrator and treasurer sections now take a name and an email address and send an invitation. The treasurer section appears only on a congregation or a new worshiping community, and the treasurer's address must belong to someone on that congregation's roll. The operator sees the same "invitation sent" message whether or not the address already has an account, so the page never reveals who is registered. Each open invitation shows its delivery state (Sent, Delivered, or "Bounced. Check the address"), and the operator can resend it, revoke it, or revoke every open invitation for the organization at once.

**Receiving one.** The email comes from PresbyPortal in the same layout as the platform's other emails, greets the person by first name, names the congregation, and carries a button plus the plain link. The link always points at the PresbyPortal site, never at a church's own domain. It works for 7 days by default.

**Accepting.** Opening the link shows who the invitation is for (with the email address partly hidden) and what it grants, and changes nothing by itself: reloading the page or opening it twice is harmless.
- **Someone without an account** sets a password, then sets up two-step sign-in, and lands in the congregation's portal.
- **Someone with an account** signs in (with their two-step code if they use one) and presses **Accept**. Nothing is granted without that click.
- **Signed in as someone else?** The page says so and offers to switch accounts.
- **A used, expired, revoked or replaced link** shows one plain "this link isn't active" page, identical in every case.

**Safety built in.**
- The link works once. Resending makes a new link and kills the old one.
- The link never appears in a web address or in the audit log. Once the email has been delivered (or has failed, or been cancelled), the stored copy of the message is replaced with a note saying the link was removed, and the email-queue "Retry" button refuses such a message and points to Resend instead. The link is stored only while the message is waiting to be sent.
- Limits stop runaway sending: at most 3 invitations a day to any one address (across all organizations), 10 a day per organization, 20 a day per operator, one resend every 5 minutes, and 5 resends per invitation.
- Every invitation sent, resent, revoked, accepted or refused is recorded in the audit log; the recorded details never include the link, the password or the invited address.
- **The operator may accept an invitation sent to their own address**, for a congregation they will run themselves. It goes through exactly the same link, address check and Accept click as anyone else's, is recorded as self-designated, and shows a "Self-designated" badge on the organization page. Being a platform operator grants nothing inside a congregation by itself.

#### What changes for people using the platform today

- Operators: the "sign up first" instruction is gone from the organization page; send an invitation instead.
- Anyone signed in with no congregation now sees "If you were invited, open the link in your invitation email." on the no-organization page.
- Nothing changes for existing administrators, treasurers or members.

#### What this release does not do, and what you should know

- **A congregation's own administrator cannot yet invite staff to sign in.** The second person a founding administrator adds through Staff still has no login. That is the next piece of work (Invitations Increment 2).
- **New invitees hit a known problem when they first set up two-step sign-in.** After setup, the recovery codes appear and then disappear within about a second, and the page loses track of where the person was going, so they enter one more two-step code before they land in the portal. Copy the recovery codes the moment they appear; if they vanish, reloading the two-step page within five minutes shows them again. This problem predates invitations, and a fix is next in line, before anyone other than the operator is invited.
- **Accepting creates permanent records.** Accepting an invitation adds a person, a membership and their access to the congregation, and these cannot be deleted. Test on a congregation made for testing, not on a real one.
- **Google sign-in is not part of accepting yet.** An invitee creates a password or signs in to an existing account; signing in with Google to accept is a later step.
- **Invited addresses are kept.** The design leaves room to clear an invited address 90 days after the invitation ends, but that clean-up is not built yet.
- **Other emails that carry sign-in links** (password reset, email-address change, statistics-report invitations) are not yet removed from storage after sending; only invitation emails are.
- **Delivery states need the email webhook.** Without the webhook secret configured, an invitation shows "Sent" and never "Delivered" or "Bounced".
- **Setup required before use:** the database change for this release is applied by hand before the release goes live (`docs/deployment.md` → "Pre-deploy runbook: 0059").

#### New Routes

- `/invite` — the invitation page (public; works signed in or not)
- `/invite/accepted` — the confirmation after accepting
