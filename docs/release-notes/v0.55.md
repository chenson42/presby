## 0.55.0 — 2026-11-09

### Feature: Worship scheduling

**Value:** Your congregation can now schedule music teams, ushers, greeters and liturgical roles from PresbyPortal instead of a spreadsheet and a string of emails. Worship is off until an administrator turns it on under "Features". Congregations and new worshiping communities can use it.

#### What's New

**Teams and positions.** An administrator creates worship teams, each with the positions it fills (for example Vocals, Guitar, Greeter), and adds people to a team. Only people with a current record at your congregation can be added, so a new congregation adds its people first. For each person you say which positions they can fill. A ready-made team can be used as a starting point.

**Services.** A service is a date on your calendar with the teams that serve at it. It appears on your members' calendar. You can add one service, or save a service type for a weekly service and create several services from it. Cancelling a service takes it off the calendar, closes open requests, and emails the people who were asked or scheduled.

**Asking people to serve.** A leader places people in positions on a service, then presses Send. Until Send, nobody is told. Each person gets one email listing their dates and positions, signs in, and accepts or declines. A leader can also record an answer on someone's behalf, and can add a guest who is not on file. A guest is not emailed.

**Dates people can't serve.** Anyone on a team can save dates they can't serve. When a leader is choosing someone, a person who saved that date shows as "Can't serve on" the date.

**Who can do what.**
- Founding administrators at congregations and new worshiping communities now hold the three worship permissions (manage teams, plan services, schedule), including those already set up.
- A "Worship Leader" role is available to give to others.
- The leader of a team can schedule that team without any extra permission. A person becomes a team's leader the same way as for any group, and in a group that also approves reimbursements, that seat does both.
- When someone declines with a reason, only the people who can schedule that team see the reason. Others see "Declined".

**Good to know.**
- Answering from the email without signing in, song lists and bulletins are not part of this release.
- People who may be under 18 are not emailed. Their leader asks a parent or guardian and records the answer.
- Reminders go out once a night, so they arrive in the late evening, US Eastern time.
- Removing someone from a service, or moving a service's time, does not send them a message yet. The dialog says so.
- The text of each worship email (position, team, date and congregation name, no one's name) is kept in the platform's mail queue, as other notices are.
- The "Worship & Service Planning" placeholder tile is gone. In its place is a "Worship" tile, shown once your congregation turns Worship on.

**Help.** Three new help pages replace the old placeholder: "Setting up worship", "Scheduling a worship team" and "Serving on a worship team". "Your first day as an administrator" now mentions worship. Each was walked against this release.
